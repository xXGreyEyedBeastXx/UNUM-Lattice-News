# UNUM Laundering Map Reference

**Status:** active external methodology pointer  
**Updated:** 2026-08-24

UNUM Lattice News keeps its own factual, causal, evidentiary, and correction records.

For reusable analysis of moral, reputational, rhetorical, historical, policy-name, motive, connection, uncertainty, and other evidentiary laundering patterns, use:

- `xXGreyEyedBeastXx/UNUM-Laundering-Map`
- `README.md` — jurisdiction and prime anti-laundering rules
- `maps/ANTI_LAUNDERING_REFERENCE_MAP_v0_1.md` — reusable pattern map
- `schemas/LAUNDERING_EVENT_v0_1.yaml` — structured case grammar

## Handoff rule

```text
News
-> preserves what happened, dates, sources, state transitions,
   claims, relations, contributions, affected populations,
   accountability state, and corrections

Laundering Map
-> preserves how a fact, omission, frame, reputation signal,
   protective function, denial, uncertainty, or historical cut
   was moved across evidentiary jurisdiction and changed the
   apparent causal or moral picture
```

Do not move an underlying News fact into the Laundering Map and then delete its evidentiary home from News.

Do not copy a favorable fact into a harm audit merely for balance.

If a favorable fact actually defeats, narrows, reroutes, or changes confidence in the scoped claim, preserve it under the exact evidence function it performs.

If it merely makes an actor look better, it is non-responsive to the scoped harm.

## Anti-amnesia lock

The existence of the separate Laundering Map does not reset News investigations to neutral.

```text
revisable determination
!= memoryless determination
```

Established causal trajectories, dates, connections, attempts, warnings, notice, resistance, outcomes, and correction history remain available until materially relevant evidence changes them.

## Tiny lock

> News traces the arrows. Laundering Map traces who moved the arrows, hid the arrows, or tried to make an unrelated arrow pay somebody else's moral bill.
