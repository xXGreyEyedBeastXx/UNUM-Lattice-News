## Purpose

This ledger exists because the higher-level clue chain is useful but can become dangerous if it compresses away the very distinctions needed for attribution, falsification, and causal reconstruction.

It preserves the high-value anchors that must remain recoverable even when the investigation is summarized:

```text
DATE / TIME
+ ACTOR / ORGANIZATIONAL SEAT
+ OBSERVED OR REPORTED CLUE
+ EVIDENCE POSTURE
+ OPERATIONAL CONSEQUENCE
+ COUNTER-READING
+ MISSING DISCRIMINATOR
+ RECOVERY SURFACE
```

Compression is allowed for orientation. It is not allowed to erase differences that could change the causal model.

## Non-compression locks

```text
SYNTHESIS != SOURCE ERASURE
SAME MECHANISM != SAME ACTOR KNOWLEDGE
SYSTEM KNOWLEDGE != IDENTICAL INDIVIDUAL KNOWLEDGE
PLAN FAMILY != SINGLE DOCUMENT IDENTITY
FOREKNOWLEDGE != EXACT-DATE-AND-SCALE KNOWLEDGE
REDEPLOYMENT INTO A SECURITY FIELD != PROOF OF EACH UNIT'S EXACT TASK
POLITICAL PRIORITY DISTORTION != DELIBERATE SACRIFICE
MODEL FAILURE != INNOCENT COINCIDENCE BY DEFAULT
COMMON CAUSAL SYSTEM != RANDOMLY INDEPENDENT EVENTS
```

## I. Long-range incursion knowledge

### 2018-04-16 — broad invasion assessment

**Actor / seat:** IDF Military Intelligence; distribution reportedly included the military secretaries of the prime minister and defense minister, National Security Council, Shin Bet, Mossad, Chief of Staff office, and other senior officers.

**Clue:** Israeli reporting in 2026 says Military Intelligence circulated an assessment asking whether Hamas was building toward a broad attack deep into Israeli territory. The scenario described approximately 3,000 fighters, attacks on Gaza Division bases, harm to civilian targets, fence breaches, and large rocket salvos.

**Prime-ministerial custody clue:** Reporting says the assessment entered the prime minister's controlled weekend-reading box under a procedure in which material placed there was expected to be read. Netanyahu's office disputes that the later `Jericho Wall` plan itself reached him and argues that security assessments minimized the feasibility of such a ground raid.

**Posture:**

- `STRATEGIC_SCENARIO_FOREKNOWLEDGE = SUPPORTED`
- `2018_PM_OFFICE_RECEIPT_OF_BROAD_INVASION_ASSESSMENT = STRONGLY_REPORTED`
- `NETANYAHU_PERSONALLY_READ_EXACT_LATER_2022_JERICHO_WALL_VERSION = NOT ESTABLISHED`

**Do not collapse:** the 2018 broad assessment and the later detailed `Jericho Wall` document are related plan surfaces, not automatically the same document.

**Recovery surface:** `research/investigations/OCT7_FOREKNOWLEDGE_LADDER_ISRAELI_INTERNAL_RECORD_2018_2026.md`

### 2022 — detailed `Jericho Wall` plan

**Actor / seat:** Israeli Military Intelligence.

**Clue:** Military Intelligence obtained a detailed Hamas invasion plan later codenamed `Jericho Wall`, describing a coordinated ground invasion, attacks on military positions and Israeli communities, and hostage-taking. Internal investigations found that the plan substantially resembled the eventual October 7 attack.

**Classification:** Senior officers treated it as aspirational or beyond Hamas's current capability.

**Posture:**

- `ASSESSMENT_THAT_PLAN_WAS_NOT_FEASIBLE = DOCUMENTED`

**Causal relevance:** The failure was not absence of a plan surface; it was the system's classification of that plan.

## II. The year before October 7 was managed instability, not simple calm

**Actor:** Hamas.

**Posture:** `VISIBLE_INVASION_STYLE_TRAINING = DOCUMENTED`

### 2023-01-26/27 — Jenin raid followed by Gaza rockets / Israeli strikes

**Clue:** After a deadly Israeli raid in Jenin, militants fired rockets from Gaza; Israel responded with airstrikes, including against a Hamas training site.

**Causal relevance:** West Bank events and Gaza-border escalation were already operationally coupled.

**Clue:** After an Israeli raid in Nablus killed 11 Palestinians, militants in Gaza fired rockets and Israel conducted airstrikes.

**Causal relevance:** Another example of violence outside Gaza producing Gaza-border escalation while the overall system still treated episodes as limited and containable.

**Clue:** Hundreds of settlers attacked Palestinian homes, shops, and vehicles. UN reporting cited in the Huwara investigation records 387 Palestinian injuries across Huwara and nearby communities, with 378 attributed to Israeli security forces and nine to settlers.

**Causal relevance:** This is a pre-October comparator for the West Bank security ecology into which later Gaza reinforcement was redeployed.

**Posture:** `ASYMMETRIC_COERCIVE_ENFORCEMENT_PATTERN = SUPPORTED`

### 2023-04-05/07 — Al-Aqsa / Gaza / Lebanon escalation

**Clue:** Israeli police entered Al-Aqsa Mosque during Ramadan/Passover tensions; rockets were fired from Gaza and a major barrage came from southern Lebanon; Israel struck Gaza and Lebanon.

**Causal relevance:** Jerusalem, West Bank, Gaza, and regional security theaters were not analytically independent.

### 2023-05-02/13 — `Operation Shield and Arrow`

**Actors:** Palestinian Islamic Jihad; Israeli military; Hamas as a relevant nonparticipant.

**Clue:** More than 100 rockets were fired on May 2; Israel launched `Operation Shield and Arrow` on May 9. The IDF states that 1,469 rockets were launched during the five-day conflict.

**High-value interpretive clue:** Hamas did not directly join the fighting.

**Later internal correction:** IDF investigations identified Hamas's restraint in such rounds as one of the observations Israel misread under its prevailing concept.

**Causal transformation:**

```text
HAMAS NONPARTICIPATION
-> interpreted as desire for quiet / deterrence
-> confidence in containment strengthened
```

**Alternative model:** Hamas restraint could also have represented strategic concealment or conservation of capability.

**Lock:** `HAMAS_NONPARTICIPATION_IN_ONE_ROUND != ABSENCE_OF_LARGE_ATTACK_INTENT`

### 2023-06-14 — economic-concession / quiet model

**Actor seat:** senior Israeli security decision environment.

**Clue:** Later Israeli reporting on internal discussions says officials considered additional economic concessions to Gaza, including labor-entry visas and infrastructure measures, with reported assessments that Hamas had no interest in Gaza violence, wanted quiet, and could be further restrained through economic incentives.

**Causal relevance:** The deterrence/containment model was not only rhetoric; it shaped economic/security policy.

**Posture:** `OPERATIONALIZED_DETERRENCE_MODEL = SUPPORTED CANDIDATE`

### Summer 2023 — technology-centric border defense

**Clue:** The approximately $1.1 billion barrier completed in 2021 depended heavily on sensors, cameras, remote systems, and fortified infrastructure. Later reporting documented that some manned functions had been replaced by remote systems.

**Causal relevance:** Confidence in technological detection and containment influenced how much warning time commanders expected to have if a ground attack occurred.

**Posture:** `TECHNOLOGICAL_CONTAINMENT_DEPENDENCE = SUPPORTED`

### 2023-09-12 — visible assault rehearsal and deterrence confidence on the same date

**Hamas clue:** Public live-fire `Strong Pillar` exercise showed fighters blasting through a replica border gate, entering in pickup trucks, destroying mock towers/communications infrastructure, moving through a mock Israeli locality, and firing at human-shaped targets.

**Israeli command clue:** Later Israeli reporting says Chief of Staff Herzi Halevi toured the Gaza border that same date and assessed that `Operation Shield and Arrow` had succeeded in deterring Hamas and that border communities were secure from an invasion.

**High-value pairing:**

```text

SAME-DATE DETERRENCE CONFIDENCE
```

This pairing must not be compressed into generic `warnings were missed`; it is an explicit contradiction between visible capability evidence and prevailing assessment.

**Posture:** `REALITY_CONTRADICTED_MODEL = STRONGLY SUPPORTED`

**Clue:** Roughly 100 soldiers in two Commando Brigade companies, deployed as Gaza-border reinforcement during the holiday period, were sent to Huwara on October 5.

**IDF counterposition:** The army argued these were General Staff reserve/reinforcement forces rather than the regular Gaza-border security complement.

**Both can be true:** Removing reinforcement can reduce surge/response capacity while leaving the nominal routine roster unchanged.

**Genuine trigger:** A Palestinian gunman fired at an Israeli family; no family member was physically injured.

**Settler / far-right escalation:** Dozens of settlers entered Huwara; Palestinian property was damaged; Religious Zionism MK Zvi Sukkot erected a sukkah on the main road; reporting says an additional infantry company was dispatched to secure Sukkot and accompanying ultranationalist settlers.

## V. Night of October 6-7 — minute-level warning sequence

This sequence must not be collapsed into `there were warning signs`.

### 23:00 — October 6

Former Shin Bet chief Ronen Bar's sworn account says Gaza Division commander/intelligence and Southern Command intelligence were phoned about unusual indicators.

### 01:33 — October 7

Bar says Southern Command was notified that a combined Shin Bet/Yamam special team was being sent south because a localized raid/infiltration scenario was considered possible.

### 03:03

Shin Bet circulated an alert to relevant bodies saying the unusual preparations could indicate Hamas offensive intentions.

### 04:30

Bar held a situational assessment.

### 05:15

Bar says he ordered Netanyahu's military secretary updated. Subsequent reporting disputes when the contact actually completed and places it closer to the attack.

### Interpretation

Bar acknowledges the warning level was wrong: the signs were read as Hamas defensive readiness or at worst preparation for a limited raid rather than mass invasion.

## VI. 2025 internal IDF correction

**Review body:** IDF expert team led by Maj. Gen. (res.) Sami Turgeman.

**Scope:** reviewed 25 military inquiries and approximately 80 relevant witnesses.

**Finding:** October 7 did not arise from an information vacuum; diverse, high-quality, unusual information existed across IDF bodies and professional analysis could and should have produced a significant operational alert.

**Additional findings preserved:**

- years-long conceptual/intelligence failures;
- failure to engage operationally with `Jericho Wall`;
- persistent mismatch between threat and force posture;
- flawed decision-making and force employment on the night;
- unusual information collected by border observers over preceding months;
- no meaningful frontline/reserve action during the critical hours;
- Nova exposed a gap in territorial-defense responsibility.

**Posture:**

- `INTERNAL_IDF_FINDING_NO_INFORMATION_VACUUM = CONFIRMED`
- `SIGNIFICANT_OPERATIONAL_ALERT_WAS_POSSIBLE_AND_REQUIRED = CONFIRMED_BY_IDF_EXPERT_REVIEW`

**Important methodological point:** This internal finding is useful because it contradicts a simple unforeseeable-surprise model. It still does not itself establish deliberate setup.

## VIII. Hypothesis ladder — preserve all rungs

### H1 — unforeseeable surprise

**Contradicted as a complete model.**

Substantial strategic, operational, capability, and short-term warning information existed.

### H2 — sincere but catastrophic model failure

**Strongly supported.**

The system repeatedly classified evidence through a deterrence/containment model and failed to update adequately.

### H3 — political-security priority distortion

**Strongly supported.**

West Bank / settler-security demands consumed coercive/protective capacity while Gaza-border vulnerability remained underweighted.

### H4 — knowingly tolerated vulnerability / calculated exposure

**Plausible and materially strengthened.**

Requires actor-specific evidence that relevant decision-makers understood the elevated risk and consciously accepted the vulnerable posture.

### H5 — operational pretext / deliberate setup

**Unresolved.**

Requires evidence that relevant actors understood the governing model was materially false or inadequate and preserved it because the resulting exposure or downstream event served a desired objective.

### H6 — calculated sacrifice

**Not established.**

Requires the additional edge that civilian deaths/captures were knowingly accepted as an instrumentally useful price or deliberately enabled for that purpose.
