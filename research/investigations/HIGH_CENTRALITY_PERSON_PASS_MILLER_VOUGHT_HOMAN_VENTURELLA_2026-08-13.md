
## CONFIRMED — current White House authority seat

White House records identify the Deputy Chief of Staff for Policy and Homeland Security Advisor as a principal recipient of national-security presidential directives. America First Legal's current leadership page identifies Stephen Miller as its co-founder and states that after January 20, 2025 he became Assistant to the President, Deputy Chief of Staff for Policy, and Homeland Security Advisor.

Sources:
- White House NSPM-12, 2026-06-12: https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/06/national-security-presidential-memorandum-nspm-12/
- America First Legal leadership: https://aflegal.org/leadership/

## CONFIRMED — Miller set a 3,000-per-day ICE arrest target

Reuters reported that Miller set an aggressive target of 3,000 immigration arrests per day in late May 2025. Reuters further reported that efforts to meet the target pushed immigration officers into more communities and businesses. A separate Reuters analysis reported that Miller pressed ICE leadership to target anyone without legal status, not only people with serious criminal convictions.

Sources:
- Reuters, 2025-07-11: https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/white-house-aide-driving-trumps-aggressive-immigration-agenda-2025-07-11/
- Reuters quantitative enforcement reporting, mirrored here: https://www.investing.com/news/world-news/trumps-ice-arrests-noncriminals-despite-crimefocused-message-4095352

Confirmed causal edge:

```text
Miller policy pressure / numerical arrest target
-> ICE leadership enforcement pressure
-> expansion of arrest activity into workplaces / communities
```

Do not convert this into attribution for every arrest, detention injury, deportation, or death. Case-level responsibility still requires operational trace.

## CONFIRMED — expansion target formally linked to P.L. 119-21

The White House republished Miller's statement that the 2025 reconciliation bill would increase by "orders of magnitude" the scope, scale, and speed of removals. The enacted law subsequently supplied very large immigration-enforcement and detention appropriations already traced elsewhere in the repository.

Source:
- White House, 2025-06-04: https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2025/06/icymi-most-essential-piece-of-legislation-in-the-western-world/

This is evidence of explicit intent to increase enforcement scale, not proof that Miller personally designed every appropriation line.

## CONFIRMED — private ideological/legal bridge exists

America First Legal states that Miller co-founded the organization in 2021. Its current leadership page still identifies him as co-founder while describing his current White House role. Historical AFL materials identify him as the organization head/president before his return to government.

Sources:
- AFL launch, 2021-04-06: https://aflegal.org/senior-trump-officials-launch-america-first-legal-foundation/
- AFL leadership: https://aflegal.org/leadership/

Do not claim current operational control over AFL absent current governance evidence. The confirmed edge is lineage / founding / ideological-network continuity.


## CONFIRMED — current OMB director

White House FY2026 budget materials identify Russ Vought as Director of the Office of Management and Budget.

Source:
- White House FY2026 budget release, 2025-05: https://www.whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/2025/05/the-white-house-office-of-management-and-budget-releases-the-presidents-fiscal-year-2026-skinny-budget/

## CONFIRMED — Project 2025 / ideological design bridge

Heritage identifies Vought as the author of Project 2025's chapter on the Executive Office of the President. Heritage also describes Project 2025 as a policy-plus-personnel transition effort intended to prepare a future conservative administration to operate federal agencies immediately.

Sources:
- Heritage Project 2025 chapter list: https://www.heritage.org/node/25155114/print-display
- Heritage Project 2025 overview: https://www.heritage.org/conservatism/commentary/project-2025

The Center for Renewing America states that it was launched in 2021 by Vought after his first tenure as OMB director and was designed to supply policy research and fight national policy battles.

Source:
- Center for Renewing America: https://americarenewing.com/about/

Confirmed topology:

```text
private ideological policy design
-> Vought
-> OMB / executive management authority
```

Do not infer that every Trump administration action matching Project 2025 was ordered by Heritage, CRA, or Vought.

## CONFIRMED — Vought used temporary CFPB control to halt supervision

Reuters reported that after becoming acting CFPB director in February 2025, Vought ordered staff to cease supervision and examination activity, closed headquarters temporarily, and said the bureau would not take its next funding draw. Reuters described the result as removing a major layer of federal oversight from consumer-facing financial firms.

Sources:
- Reuters, 2025-02-09/10: https://www.reuters.com/world/us/trumps-acting-cfpb-chief-halts-all-supervision-companies-2025-02-09/

Confirmed structural edge:

```text
Vought acting CFPB authority
-> halt supervision / examination
-> reduced federal consumer-finance oversight capacity
```

Specific consumer losses caused by the halt require case-level tracing. The reduction in oversight capacity itself is confirmed structural harm under the repository's power/oversight rubric.

## CONFIRMED — OMB used spending-release controls across agencies; legality contested

Reuters documented tens of billions of dollars in congressionally approved projects remaining frozen during early 2025 while Vought publicly argued that presidents possess authority to withhold spending. Other reporting based on court-released OMB records documented requirements that agencies submit spending plans aligned with White House directives before money was released.

Sources:
- Reuters, 2025-02-06: https://www.reuters.com/world/us/billions-dollars-us-projects-remain-frozen-by-trump-managers-advocates-say-2025-02-06/
- Washington Post, 2025-08-19: https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2025/08/19/trump-budget-congress-impoundment/

Confirmed:

```text
OMB apportionment / release authority
-> delayed or conditioned access to appropriated funds
-> project / agency implementation disruption
```

Legal conclusion: `PENDING / contested`. Do not label all withholding unlawful without claim-specific adjudication.

## PATH_FORWARD — civil-service reclassification / Vought-specific attribution

The Trump administration implemented Schedule Policy/Career in 2026, reclassifying roughly 8,000 senior policy-influencing career positions into an at-will-like category with reduced adverse-action protections. This is a confirmed structural centralization of presidential personnel leverage.

Sources:
- White House EO 14410: https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/06/implementing-schedule-policy-career-in-the-excepted-service/
- OPM Schedule Policy/Career: https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/hiring-information/hiring-authorities/schedule-policycareer/

Vought's Project 2025 / executive-power design role makes this a high-priority attribution path, but the current pass does not automatically assign the 2026 executive order to him personally.

Federal ethics-disclosure reporting reviewed by the Washington Post found that Homan received more than $5,000 in consulting fees from GEO Care, a GEO Group division, during the two years before his January 2025 government appointment. The exact amount and work scope were not disclosed in the filing.

Source:
- Washington Post, 2025-05-27: https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2025/05/27/border-czar-ethics-consulting-fees/

Counterevidence / scope lock:
- Homan stated he would recuse himself from involvement, discussion, input, or decisions concerning future government contracts.

Therefore:


The Washington Post reported that Homan championed expansion of immigration detention to at least 100,000 beds, compared with the roughly 41,500-bed funded baseline then in place. GEO and CoreCivic were positioned to supply substantial additional private detention capacity.

Source:
- Washington Post, 2025-05-27: https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2025/05/27/border-czar-ethics-consulting-fees/

Source:
- Washington Post, 2025-08-01: https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2025/08/01/ice-david-venturella-geo-immigration-detention/

# IV. David Venturella — revolving-door / detention-implementation node


## CONFIRMED — long GEO tenure immediately before return to ICE

Reuters and the Washington Post report that Venturella worked at GEO Group after earlier ICE service, served as a GEO executive from 2012 to 2023, remained a paid GEO consultant through January 31, 2025, and returned to ICE in early February 2025.

Sources:
- Reuters, 2026-05-13: https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/us-ice-official-who-worked-private-prison-firm-will-be-agencys-new-acting-head-2026-05-13/
- Washington Post, 2025-08-01: https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2025/08/01/ice-david-venturella-geo-immigration-detention/

## CONFIRMED — ethics waiver / authorization existed; initial contract-role denial retained

The Washington Post reported that DHS granted Venturella an authorization/waiver from the ordinary one-year impartiality restriction concerning matters involving a recent former employer. ICE stated in 2025 that Venturella had no role in reviewing, approving, or recommending contracts.

Source:
- Washington Post, 2025-08-01: https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2025/08/01/ice-david-venturella-geo-immigration-detention/

The existence of a waiver does not establish favoritism. It increases the importance of contract-by-contract tracing.

## CONFIRMED — detention-management authority before becoming acting director

The Washington Post reported that Venturella became the No. 2 official in the ICE division managing detention-center contracts after rejoining the agency and helped oversee the detention expansion. In May 2026 DHS announced he would become acting ICE director after Todd Lyons' departure.

Sources:
- Washington Post, 2025-08-01: https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2025/08/01/ice-david-venturella-geo-immigration-detention/
- Reuters, 2026-05-13: https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/us-ice-official-who-worked-private-prison-firm-will-be-agencys-new-acting-head-2026-05-13/

## CONFIRMED — GEO benefited materially during the expansion period

During Venturella's return to ICE, GEO received new and expanded ICE detention and monitoring business. This corporate benefit is confirmed independently in GEO filings and procurement records already in the repository.

Important boundary:

```text
GEO benefit during Venturella tenure
!= Venturella caused / steered award
```

Specific favoritism remains `PENDING` unless procurement, communications, recusal, or decision records establish it.

## CONFIRMED — later divestment / recusal narrows current conflict surface

By August 2026 Venturella reported that he had divested GEO stock and recused himself from GEO-related detention contracts/obligations and specified company matters. This materially changes the current conflict analysis and must be retained.

Source:
- Wall Street Journal, 2026-08-03: https://www.wsj.com/politics/policy/acting-ice-chief-divests-detention-stocks-and-recuses-from-contracts-589f10db

This does not erase earlier conflict questions; it narrows the forward-looking scope.
