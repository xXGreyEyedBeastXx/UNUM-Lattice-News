## Purpose

This file treats the Trump administration as a system-level investigative object without presupposing a verdict. Individual scandals and actor dossiers are subordinate evidence trees. The central question is whether recurring observed outcomes are better explained by legitimate policy objectives and ordinary political patronage, or by a persistent architecture of private enrichment, preferential access, institutional capture, coercion, retaliation, and protection.

Every major mechanism carries three seats: strongest harm/capture model, strongest legitimate-governance model, and discriminators capable of changing the conclusion.

Do not convert shared nodes into direct edges. Do not convert allegations into findings. Do not convert legal non-liability into factual exoneration. Do not erase beneficial outcomes because an actor is otherwise harmful.

## Probe A — Presidential information -> private monetization -> market advantage

Trump Media & Technology Group announced Truth API in July 2026 as a licensed B2B feed providing real-time access to posts from high-ranking Truth Social accounts. TMTG explicitly described the product as a new recurring revenue stream. Independent reporting subsequently identified high-frequency trading firms among customers paying substantial monthly fees for rapid access to influential Truth Social content. Presidential communications can be market-moving.

```text
Donald Trump / presidential communication
        |
        v
Truth Social / Trump Media
        |
        +--> ordinary public distribution
        |
        +--> paid low-latency institutional feed
                    |
                    v
             financial traders
                    |
                    v
            possible market advantage

Trump Media <--- subscription revenue
```

**Harm/conflict model:** presidential authority can generate market-significant information while a privately affiliated platform monetizes distribution speed. Structural conflict is documented; deliberate market manipulation or intentional preferential disclosure is not established.

**Legitimate/business model:** low-latency feeds are ordinary financial-data products; commercialization can be commercially rational and lawful.

**Discriminators:** actual latency; whether official policy announcements traverse the paid path first; subscriber trading behavior; separation policies; Trump's retained financial benefit; competitor treatment.

## Probe B — Family/appointee crypto -> public policy -> private wealth

Trump's disclosures and independent reporting identify very large Trump-family crypto income while the administration has pursued materially more crypto-friendly policy and enforcement. A further seam requiring population-level analysis is crypto ownership among administration appointees themselves.

```text
crypto holdings / ventures
        ^                 |
        |                 v
political appointees -> regulatory / enforcement authority
        ^                 |
        |                 v
Trump-family ventures <- sector opportunity/value
```

**Harm/conflict model:** unusually large direct financial exposure exists inside an administration changing the industry's regulatory environment. This creates persistent conflicts even when individual policies have legitimate rationales.

**Legitimate-policy model:** regulatory clarity can promote innovation, reduce offshore migration, and resolve jurisdictional ambiguity. Industry-wide benefit does not establish private-purpose causation.

**Discriminators:** date-level policy/asset series; decisions uniquely beneficial to Trump-linked assets; investor identities; differential treatment of competitors; recusals/ethics screens; policy actions adverse to insider holdings.

## Probe C — Tariffs/trade disruption -> farm distress -> government relief -> political credit

USDA announced $12 billion in Farmer Bridge Assistance, with $11 billion in row-crop bridge payments explicitly responding to temporary trade-market disruptions and increased production costs. USDA attributes the underlying distress principally to prior Biden-era policies, unfair foreign competition, persistent inflation, and market disruption. USDA also expanded disaster assistance and commodity-loan support in 2026.

```text
trade/tariff strategy
       |
       v
retaliation + market reordering + input effects
       |
       v
farm revenue/cost pressure
       |
       v
federal bridge / commodity / disaster payments
       |
       +--> short-term stabilization
       +--> administration political credit
       +--> fiscal/socialized cost
```

**Harm/self-reinforcing model:** to the extent administration tariffs materially create export loss or input pressure, later relief can socialize policy costs while creating political credit for rescue.

**Legitimate-strategy model:** temporary disruption can be an anticipated cost of renegotiating unfavorable trade relationships; bridge payments can preserve productive capacity while new market access develops. Farm weakness also has commodity-cycle, weather, interest-rate, inflation, and prior-policy causes.

**Discriminators:** commodity-level exports; retaliatory exposure; attributable input costs; bankruptcies/delinquencies; payment distribution by farm size; durable new market access; tariff revenue versus relief and downstream costs; less-exposed controls.

## Probe D — Clemency -> network proximity / protection

DOJ's grants and denials provide comparison populations. Independent investigative reporting has already demonstrated that clemency outcomes can be represented as an influencer network rather than a sequence of isolated anecdotes.

For each individualized recipient, type edges to campaign/PAC donations, Trump-family/business payments, club relationships, paid clemency advocates, political work, media advocacy, crypto investment, foreign-government relationships, ideological organizations, personal relationships, and post-clemency benefits.

**Harm/protection model:** disproportionate association between grants and money/access/loyalty would support patronage/protection.

**Legitimate-clemency model:** presidents historically use broad pardon authority for mercy, perceived overreach, sentencing disparities, political offenses, and cases surfaced by advocates; access can help deserving cases reach decision-makers without purchasing outcomes.

**Discriminators:** matched grants/denials; quantified network edges; disconnected grants; connected denials; lobbying payments; categorical versus individualized pardons.

## Probe E — Oversight capacity as a system variable

A major seam is not merely whether conflicts occur, but whether the institutions designed to detect and constrain them remain independent and capable.

Federal Inspectors General possess explicit authority to investigate suspected conflict-of-interest and government-ethics violations. Therefore changes to IG staffing, independence, access, or leadership directly alter the system's capacity to detect misconduct.

The second Trump administration removed numerous inspectors general early in the term and changed ethics-governance conditions relative to the prior administration. At the same time, oversight did not disappear: GAO, courts, congressional actors, remaining inspectors, career officials, journalists, and civil society continue to produce constraints and evidence.

```text
potential misconduct/conflict
        |
        v
oversight capacity
   |          |
 strong      weakened
   |          |
 detection   lower detection / delayed correction
   |          |
 correction  greater executive discretion
```

**Capture model:** weakening or replacing independent oversight increases the probability that conflicts, procurement irregularities, retaliation, and misuse of authority survive without correction; later benefits to insiders become more probative if they follow the removal of relevant constraints.

**Executive-control model:** presidents can legitimately seek accountable, competent executive-branch oversight rather than officials viewed as partisan, insubordinate, ineffective, or inherited from prior administrations. Removal alone does not prove concealment.

**Discriminators:** reason and timing for each removal; whether replacements possess relevant audit/investigative competence; vacancies and acting tenures; investigations pending at removal; subsequent handling of those investigations; whistleblower outcomes; comparison with prior administrations; measurable changes in findings, referrals, access, and correction rates.

**Method rule:** do not encode `oversight official removed -> underlying allegation true`. Encode the removal as a change in detection/constraint capacity and test downstream consequences.

## Probe F — Defense advisory power -> venture capital -> Trump-family investment network

A newly surfaced 2026 seam requires primary-record development. Independent reporting identifies Defense Policy Board appointments involving Marc Andreessen and Blake Masters. Both have relationships to Andreessen Horowitz; a16z co-invests in defense/space technology companies with 1789 Capital, where Donald Trump Jr. is a partner. Companies in the overlapping investment ecosystem include firms receiving substantial federal defense/space business.

                                              |
                                              v
                              strategic advice / priorities
                                              |

**Current status:** structural conflict lead, not proof of procurement manipulation or policy capture.

**Capture model:** private investors tied through co-investment and family relationships to the president can gain advisory proximity to strategic defense decision-making while portfolio ecosystems benefit from expanding federal defense priorities.

**Expertise model:** successful technology investors can provide valuable knowledge about innovation, industrial capacity, AI, autonomy, manufacturing, and emerging threats; overlapping investments are expected in a concentrated defense-tech sector and do not prove biased advice.

**Discriminators:** official appointment records and ethics terms; recusals; holdings; specific advice/recommendations; procurement chronology; portfolio exposure before and after appointments; differential treatment of competitors; whether board members influence acquisition decisions or only broad strategy; policy decisions adverse to their portfolios.

This lane should be cross-linked to Hegseth's actor dossier but kept independent of the LaVena Johnson lead.

## Probe G — Immigration enforcement -> contracting -> political/network benefit

DHS/ICE/CBP contracting remains a high-value unfinished lane. Public contract data can support a population-level map of detention, transportation, surveillance, construction, communications, technology, and removal-related spending.

Do not begin with selected politically connected contractors. Build the award population first, then enrich it with ownership, prior employers, donations, lobbying, former-government personnel, subcontractors, and performance history.

```text
immigration policy / executive orders
          |
          v
DHS / ICE / CBP operational demand
          |
          v
procurement / grants / contracts
          |
          +--> operational capacity
          +--> corporate revenue
          +--> workforce / infrastructure expansion
          |
          v
human + fiscal outcomes
```

**Capture model:** emergency/accelerated enforcement can create large procurement opportunities where political proximity, former-employer relationships, donor status, or compressed bidding may influence awards.

**Operational model:** rapid policy changes require rapid acquisition of real capacity; politically connected firms may still win because they are qualified, available, or competitively priced.

**Discriminators:** competition level; bid windows; sole-source/urgent awards; price comparisons; performance; political and employment edges; award modifications; independent procurement reviews; connected firms that lost bids; disconnected firms that won major awards.

## Cross-probe topology emerging

The expanded pass reveals a recurring boundary problem, not one proven master conspiracy:

```text
PUBLIC AUTHORITY
      |
      +--> information
      +--> regulation/enforcement
      +--> trade policy
      +--> relief spending
      +--> clemency
      +--> appointments/advice
      +--> procurement
      |
      v
BOUNDARY / ROUTER
      |
      +--> family businesses
      +--> private platforms
      +--> investment networks
      +--> industry networks
      +--> political allies
      +--> contractors
      +--> affected constituencies
      |
      v
PRIVATE / POLITICAL / MATERIAL BENEFIT

             ^
             |
      OVERSIGHT CAPACITY
   constrains / detects / corrects
```

The new insight is that **oversight is not merely another actor node. It is a conductance-control layer.** Removing, weakening, bypassing, or strengthening oversight changes how easily every other public-to-private pathway can operate.

This yields a stronger system hypothesis:

> The relevant object may be less `Trump -> favors` than a coupled architecture of **routing + constraint modification**: public authority creates or redirects flows while oversight institutions determine the resistance those flows encounter.

This is a hypothesis to test, not a conclusion.

## Cross-system discriminators

The capture model should lose weight if we observe substantial numbers of:

- decisions materially adverse to Trump-family financial interests;
- politically disconnected firms outperforming connected firms in procurement under transparent competition;
- strongly connected clemency applicants being denied while disconnected comparable applicants are granted;
- independent inspectors and ethics officials retained despite findings adverse to the administration;
- Trump-linked crypto assets receiving no differential regulatory advantage;
- farm/trade gains exceeding relief/socialized costs over an appropriate horizon;
- advisory-board recommendations harming members' portfolio interests;
- administration enforcement against major donors/allies on comparable terms.

The capture model should gain weight when **chronology + material benefit + preferential treatment + weakened constraint + repeated topology** converge in independent domains.

## Method lock

This system case is not a declaration that Donald Trump or his administration constitutes a criminal enterprise, dictatorship, conspiracy, or corrupt administration. Those are conclusions requiring defined criteria and evidence.

Likewise, electoral victory, legal authority, absence of prosecution, or institutional approval does not establish that conduct is ethical, beneficial, truthful, or free of corruption.

The lattice should be capable of returning allegation supported, weakened, disproven, legal-but-conflicted, harmful-but-legitimately-motivated, beneficial-despite-suspect-motives, ordinary systemic American corruption, Trump-specific intensification, or insufficient evidence.

The objective is not to make Trump look bad or good. The objective is to make the causal topology difficult to lie about.
