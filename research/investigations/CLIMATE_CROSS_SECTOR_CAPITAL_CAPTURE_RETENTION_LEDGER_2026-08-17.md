## Retained whole-circuit hypotheses

### H0 — independent / ordinary diversification model

```text
large diversified managers
-> broad index exposure across most sectors
-> repeated ownership names
-> no strategically meaningful cross-sector coordination
```

This model remains live and explains some ownership overlap.

### H1 — governance-aware portfolio optimization model

```text
large diversified managers
-> voting / stewardship / allocation capacity
-> climate-risk models reveal winners and losers
-> portfolios and engagements adapt across sectors
-> no need to desire climate harm
```

This model is strongly compatible with current evidence.

### H2 — portfolio-level climate moral-hazard model

```text
capital exposed to causal fossil system
AND
capital exposed to repair / scarcity / adaptation beneficiaries
AND
physical/public losses can be externalized
-> weaker portfolio-level incentive to eliminate the causal harm than victim-level welfare would imply
```

Supported working hypothesis; net-benefit magnitude unmeasured.

### H3 — cross-sector climate rent-extraction model

```text
known climate forcing persists
-> damage/scarcity grows
-> concentrated or owned consequence-side seats capture rent/revenue/appreciation
-> capital is reseated through diversified owners / parent firms
```

Supported as a circuit worth measuring; actor-specific causal responsibility varies by edge.

### H4 — deliberate damage-generation-for-downstream-profit model

```text
actor forecasts downstream benefit from additional warming
-> actor intentionally preserves/increases warming because of that benefit
-> downstream repair/scarcity revenue is a motivating causal objective
```

**CURRENT STATE: UNRESOLVED / NOT ESTABLISHED.**

Required promotion evidence includes internal strategy documents, board/investment-committee records, communications, lobbying/capital-allocation records, or equivalent actor-specific evidence that connects additional warming to desired downstream returns as a reason for preserving the causal mechanism.

## Anti-collapse final lock

The following findings survive even if H4 fails:

- fossil-industry climate knowledge;
- cross-sector institutional ownership overlap;
- real voting/stewardship capacity;
- direct conglomerate integration;
- storm-damage replacement demand;
- farmland scarcity as an investor value thesis;
- food/input concentration;
- sophisticated climate-risk/opportunity modeling;
- the possibility of portfolio-level hedging/capture while vulnerable populations bear nondiversifiable physical losses.

The following does **not** become true merely because the above converge:

- every asset manager position is centrally directed;
- every harmful event is caused by BlackRock, Vanguard, Koch, or another named actor;
- every climate consequence was desired;
- every downstream profit proves upstream causation.

```text
RETAIN THE CONVERGENCE.
RETAIN THE COUNTERMODEL.
RETAIN THE MISSING EDGE.
DO NOT MAKE ANY ONE OF THEM ERASE THE OTHERS.
```
