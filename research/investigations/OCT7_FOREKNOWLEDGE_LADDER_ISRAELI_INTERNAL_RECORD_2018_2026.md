# October 7 Foreknowledge Ladder — Israeli Internal Record — 2018-2026

Status: ACTIVE INVESTIGATION / MATERIAL ATTACK-RISK FOREKNOWLEDGE SUPPORTED / EXACT-DATE-AND-SCALE FOREKNOWLEDGE NOT ESTABLISHED

## Root question

What did Israeli military, intelligence and political actors themselves say or record about advance knowledge of a Hamas ground-incursion scenario before October 7, 2023?

## Why a ladder is required

`FOREKNOWLEDGE` must not be collapsed into one binary.

Distinguish:

1. `STRATEGIC_SCENARIO_FOREKNOWLEDGE` — knowledge that Hamas had developed a large ground-invasion concept.
2. `OPERATIONAL_PLAN_FOREKNOWLEDGE` — possession of a detailed plan resembling the eventual attack.
3. `CAPABILITY_SHIFT_WARNING` — evidence that the plan had moved from aspiration toward practiced capability.
4. `IMMINENT_OR_NEAR_TERM_ATTACK_WARNING` — warning that offensive action may occur soon.
5. `EXACT_DATE_TIME_SCALE_FOREKNOWLEDGE` — knowledge of the precise October 7 date, hour and full scale.

The first four can be supported without the fifth.

## 2018: broad invasion plan reached the Israeli intelligence system and, according to 2026 Israeli reporting, Netanyahu's office

Ynet/Yedioth investigative reporting by Ronen Bergman and Yuval Rubovitch, published February 10, 2026, states that on April 16, 2018, IDF Military Intelligence distributed a special assessment asking whether Hamas was building toward a broad attack deep into Israeli territory.

The assessment described approximately 3,000 fighters, attacks on Gaza Division bases, harm to civilian targets, fence breaches and large rocket salvos. It was distributed to the military secretaries of the prime minister and defense minister, the National Security Council, heads of Shin Bet and Mossad, the chief of staff's office and other senior officers.

The reporting further states that the document entered the Prime Minister's controlled weekend-reading box, under a procedure in which Netanyahu and his military secretary had agreed that material placed there was material the prime minister was required to read. Netanyahu's office disputes that the Jericho Wall plan itself reached him and says security assessments minimized the feasibility of a broad ground raid.

Correct posture:

`2018_PM_OFFICE_RECEIPT_OF_BROAD_INVASION_ASSESSMENT = STRONGLY_REPORTED`

`NETANYAHU_PERSONALLY_READ_EXACT_LATER_2022_JERICHO_WALL_VERSION = NOT_ESTABLISHED`

Do not collapse different versions of the Hamas plan into one document identity.

## 2022: detailed Jericho Wall plan obtained

Israeli Military Intelligence obtained a detailed Hamas invasion plan later codenamed `Jericho Wall`. Internal investigations found that it described a coordinated ground invasion, attacks on military positions and Israeli communities, and hostage-taking. The plan substantially resembled the eventual October 7 attack.

The major failure was not absence of the plan but classification: senior officers treated it as an aspirational or unrealistic scenario beyond Hamas's current capability.

Correct posture:

`OPERATIONAL_PLAN_FOREKNOWLEDGE = SUPPORTED`

`ASSESSMENT_THAT_PLAN_WAS_NOT_FEASIBLE = DOCUMENTED`

## 2023: capability warnings accumulated

Unit 8200 and Gaza-border intelligence personnel observed Hamas training that increasingly matched Jericho Wall.

A September 19, 2023 intelligence dossier reportedly described large-scale invasion training, including attacks on Israeli towns and military posts and hostage-taking preparations.

A Unit 8200 NCO then sent an urgent email days before October 7 warning that Jericho Wall was no longer merely theoretical but an operational, practiced plan. She wrote that the "sword is coming," that it might already be too late to prevent the event, and that plans should be prepared to reduce the damage. Senior Gaza Division intelligence officers dismissed the warning.

Correct posture:

`CAPABILITY_SHIFT_WARNING = STRONGLY_SUPPORTED`

`INTERNAL_WARNING_OF_NEAR_TERM_DANGER = SUPPORTED`

## Night of October 6-7: the system detected possible offensive intent

Former Shin Bet chief Ronen Bar's sworn 2025 affidavit provides a minute-level internal account:

- 23:00 Oct. 6 — Gaza Division commander/intelligence and Southern Command intelligence were phoned about unusual indicators.
- 01:33 Oct. 7 — Bar says Southern Command was notified of the decision to send a combined Shin Bet/Yamam special team south because a localized raid/infiltration scenario was considered possible.
- 03:03 — Shin Bet circulated an alert to relevant bodies stating that the unusual preparations could indicate Hamas offensive intentions.
- 04:30 — Bar held a situational assessment.
- 05:15 — Bar says he ordered Netanyahu's military secretary updated; subsequent reporting disputes when that contact actually occurred and places the completed contact much closer to the attack.

Bar expressly acknowledges that the warning level assigned by Shin Bet was wrong: the intelligence picture was interpreted as Hamas defensive readiness or, at worst, preparation for a limited raid rather than a mass invasion.

Correct posture:

`HOURS_BEFORE_ATTACK_OFFENSIVE_INTENT_WARNING = SUPPORTED`

`EXPECTED_SCALE = MATERIALLY_UNDERESTIMATED`

`EXACT_FULL_SCALE_FOREKNOWLEDGE = NOT_ESTABLISHED`

## 2025 IDF expert review: attack did not emerge from an information vacuum

The IDF expert team led by Maj. Gen. (res.) Sami Turgeman reviewed 25 military inquiries and approximately 80 relevant witnesses.

Its official November 2025 summary states that the October 7 surprise "did not emerge from an information vacuum — quite the opposite": diverse, high-quality and unusual information existed across IDF bodies on the night of the attack, and professional analysis could and should have produced a significant operational alert.

The team further found:

- years-long conceptual and intelligence failures;
- failure to engage operationally with Jericho Wall;
- a persistent mismatch between threat and force posture;
- flawed decision-making and force employment the night of October 7;
- unusual information collected by border observers during the preceding months;
- no meaningful action by frontline or reserve forces during the critical hours before the attack;
- Nova reflected a gap in the concept of territorial-defense responsibility.

This is the strongest internal institutional correction to the claim that the attack was simply unforeseeable.

Correct posture:

`INTERNAL_IDF_FINDING_NO_INFORMATION_VACUUM = CONFIRMED`

`SIGNIFICANT_OPERATIONAL_ALERT_WAS_POSSIBLE_AND_REQUIRED = CONFIRMED_BY_IDF_EXPERT_REVIEW`

## Relevance to Nova / calculated exposure

This evidence materially strengthens the `KNOWINGLY_TOLERATED_VULNERABILITY` / `CALCULATED_EXPOSURE` hypothesis because Nova was an approved mass civilian event near Gaza while:

- the system possessed the invasion scenario and detailed plan;
