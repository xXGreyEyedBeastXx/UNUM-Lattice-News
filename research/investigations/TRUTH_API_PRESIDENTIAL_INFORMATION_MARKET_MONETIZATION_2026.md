## The mechanism that is already established

President Donald Trump routinely uses Truth Social to communicate policy and political decisions that can move financial markets, including tariffs, trade threats, military and geopolitical developments, energy policy, and comments about individual companies.

Trump Media & Technology Group now sells `Truth API`, a high-priced machine-readable feed aimed at institutional and high-frequency traders seeking faster receipt and processing of posts from the platform's highest-profile accounts, including Trump.

That produces a documented relation:

```text
presidential authority generates market-moving information
-> president communicates through family-linked private platform
-> platform sells premium speed of access / processing
-> sophisticated market actors buy that speed because time has trading value
-> private company in which Trump has major financial exposure receives revenue
```

The public/private conflict exists even before a specific subscriber trade is proved.

## What is not yet established

The current public record reviewed here does not yet prove:

```text
named subscriber
-> received a named Trump post before ordinary public availability
-> executed a named trade because of that receipt
-> earned a measured profit
-> possessed legally nonpublic information
-> satisfied insider-trading or market-manipulation elements
```

Those are downstream questions, not reasons to erase the upstream mechanism.

## Why milliseconds can matter

A message can be publicly posted while still arriving through different technical routes at different speeds.

For high-frequency trading, the commercially relevant variable may be:

```text
publication
-> data-feed delivery
-> parsing
-> machine classification
-> order generation
-> exchange arrival
```

rather than whether an ordinary human user could eventually read the same post.

Therefore:

```text
publicly viewable information
!= economically equal access in time
```

That does not automatically make the speed differential illegal. It makes it measurable and consequential.

## Trade-policy relevance

Tariff announcements are especially important because presidential statements can immediately change expectations for currencies, manufacturers, commodities, importers, exporters, and country-specific assets.

The investigation should preserve every relevant announcement as a temporal event:

```text
policy decision / intent state
-> drafting or post creation where recoverable
-> paid-feed timestamp
-> ordinary-public timestamp
-> market movement
-> subscriber positioning and trades
-> later formal government action
```

Do not substitute the market move itself for proof of who knew what when.

## Separate live-government-matter payment edge

A distinct 2026 conflict belongs beside, but must not be merged with, Truth API:

```text
Base Co. LTD payment to Trump holding company
-> Base linked as major investor to Korea Aluminium
-> Korea Aluminium has live Commerce Department tariff/circumvention matter
```

The payment and live government matter are documented. Intervention, quid pro quo, bribery, or favorable disposition are not established by that fact alone.

This matters because the larger topology contains multiple ways public authority and private benefit can intersect:

```text
sell faster access to policy information
receive private payment from party linked to a live government matter
hold family investments in policy-sensitive industries
own policy-sensitive crypto assets
```

These are different causal mechanisms and must stay separately traceable.

## Criminality and governance

The Lattice must not use a narrow legal gap as a semantic eraser.

If current securities law ultimately does not classify the service as insider trading, the following can still be independently true and subject to evidence:

```text
presidential communications are market-moving
private presidential-family-linked company monetizes access speed
wealthy market actors can purchase a technical advantage unavailable to ordinary citizens
public authority is contributing directly to private revenue
```

The legal question and the governance/conflict question are distinct seats.
