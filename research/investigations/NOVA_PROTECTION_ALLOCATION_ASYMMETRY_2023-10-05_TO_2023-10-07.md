# Nova Protection-Allocation Asymmetry — 2023-10-05 to 2023-10-07

Status: ACTIVE INVESTIGATION / SECURITY-PRIORITY ASYMMETRY SUPPORTED / INTENTIONAL SACRIFICE NOT ESTABLISHED

## Question

What does the Israeli military's allocation of forces immediately before October 7 reveal about security priorities between the Gaza border / Nova festival and the occupied West Bank, especially Huwara and far-right Religious-Zionist settler activity?

## Findings

### 1. Two Commando Brigade companies were moved from the Gaza border to the West Bank on October 5

Multiple Israeli and international reports state that two Commando Brigade companies, roughly 100 soldiers, which had been deployed as reinforcement on the Gaza border during the holiday period, were sent to Huwara in the occupied West Bank two days before the October 7 attack.

The IDF's public response did not deny the movement. It argued that these were General Staff 'reserve' / reinforcement forces rather than the troops performing routine Gaza-border security, and said the decision would be investigated.

Correct typing:

`GAZA_BORDER_REINFORCEMENT -> REDEPLOYED_TO_HUWARA`

`IDF_COUNTERPOSITION -> ROUTINE_GAZA_SECURITY_COMPLEMENT_UNCHANGED`

These propositions can both be true. A backup force can be removed while the nominal routine-security roster remains numerically unchanged.

### 2. Huwara on October 5 was simultaneously a Palestinian-attack site and a far-right settler confrontation

A Palestinian gunman fired at an Israeli family in Huwara on October 5; no one in the family was injured. Later that day, dozens of Israeli settlers entered Huwara, Palestinian property was damaged, and clashes followed.

Religious Zionism MK Zvi Sukkot erected a sukkah on Huwara's main road in protest after the shooting. Reporting states that an additional infantry company was dispatched to secure Sukkot and the ultranationalist settlers who joined him.

Therefore the correct causal description is not simply:

`100_COMMANDOS_SENT_ONLY_TO_PROTECT_FAR_RIGHT_SETTLERS`

The stronger supported model is:

`WEST_BANK_SECURITY_ESCALATION / HUWARA`
`+ FAR_RIGHT_SETTLER_MOBILIZATION REQUIRING MILITARY_PROTECTION`
`-> ADDITIONAL_FORCE_DEMAND_IN_HUWARA`

The exact tasking order for the two Commando Brigade companies versus the separately reported additional infantry company must remain distinct until a unit-level deployment record resolves it.

### 3. Nova was approved by the IDF but received no dedicated IDF protection

The Gaza Division / Northern Brigade approved the festival only days before October 7. Later reporting and the IDF's own 2025 investigation found:

- no dedicated military force was placed at or near Nova to secure the event;
- no military representative was assigned to liaise with the police/security command post;
- Gaza-border troops were not informed that more than 3,500 people were gathered at the festival;
- the IDF did not adjust regional defenses for the event;
- no military assessment specifically addressed the mass civilian event;
- overnight warning signs on October 6-7 were discussed without Nova being raised;
- when attackers entered the festival area, there were no armed military forces present there.

The festival did have police and private/unarmed security personnel. Therefore:

`NO_DEDICATED_IDF_SECURITY != LITERALLY_NO_SECURITY_PERSONNEL`

but:

`NOVA_MILITARY_PROTECTION_GAP = DOCUMENTED`

### 4. Brigade-level concern about insufficient forces was known before approval

Reporting on the 2025 IDF probe states that the Gaza Division Northern Brigade commander had raised concern over the lack of available troops for the weekend, but Southern Command ultimately approved the festival.

This makes force availability part of the approval-chain evidence rather than a purely retrospective observation.

## Protection-allocation asymmetry

The evidence supports a first-class relation:

`PROTECTION_ALLOCATION_ASYMMETRY`

Definition:

> A powerful institution allocates scarce security capacity unevenly across populations, events, territories or political constituencies in a way that changes practical exposure to harm, whether or not discriminatory intent is independently established.

Current specimen:

```text
Gaza border assessed as containable / lower immediate threat
    -> backup commando reinforcement removed to West Bank
    -> Huwara receives heightened military attention amid shooting, settler mobilization and far-right political presence

Nova mass civilian event approved near Gaza border
    -> no dedicated IDF force
    -> no military liaison
    -> local border troops not informed event existed
    -> no tailored defense adjustment
    -> overnight warning indicators do not trigger event warning or evacuation
    -> IDF later finds it failed to defend festival-goers
```

This does not establish deliberate abandonment, advance knowledge of the Hamas attack, or an intention to sacrifice Nova attendees.

It does establish that immediately before October 7 the Israeli security system assigned marginal/reinforcement capacity toward the West Bank while leaving an approved mass civilian gathering near Gaza without dedicated military protection or even integration into the local military picture.

## Priority inference

Supported inference:

`RESOURCE_ALLOCATION -> REVEALED_SECURITY_PRIORITY`

A security institution's resource choices are evidence of what it assessed as requiring protection at that moment. The October 5-7 allocation therefore supports the conclusion that West Bank security demands were prioritized over additional Gaza-border reinforcement under the prevailing threat assessment.

The far-right settler dimension increases the political significance because military resources were demonstrably required to secure ultranationalist settler/political activity in Huwara at the same time. It does not by itself prove that political favoritism caused the original two-company redeployment.

## Relevance to Nova organizer / ideology investigation

This finding should not be used to imply that Nova's organizers were anti-government, anti-Zionist, or deliberately disfavored because of a peace-oriented festival ethos without evidence.

However, it creates a testable political question:

> Did the state allocate more immediate coercive/protective capacity to the occupied West Bank and settler-security field than to a large, state-approved civilian countercultural event near the Gaza border?

Current answer: **yes at the level of the documented October 5-7 force posture, with causation for each individual redeployment still requiring unit-level trace.**

## Locks

- Redeployment to Huwara != proof the two Commando companies were individually tasked to protect Zvi Sukkot.
- Huwara had a genuine armed-attack/security context in addition to far-right settler mobilization.
- Backup-force removal != proof routine Gaza-border troop count changed.
- Routine troop count unchanged != no loss of available reinforcement capacity.
- No dedicated IDF force at Nova != literally no police/private security.
- Protection-allocation asymmetry != intentional sacrifice.
- Security-priority failure != advance knowledge of the October 7 attack.
- Nova victim status and Hamas responsibility for the massacre remain intact.
- Resource allocation is evidence of institutional priority even when malicious intent is not established.
