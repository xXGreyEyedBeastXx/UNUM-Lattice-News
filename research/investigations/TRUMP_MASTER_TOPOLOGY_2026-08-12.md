## XII. What the compilation reveals

The merged evidence does not presently support one simple verdict such as `everything is corruption` or `everything is fascism`.

It does support a stronger cross-domain question:

> Does the Trump system repeatedly convert superior control over chokepoints — money, custody, law enforcement, information, appointments, foreign policy, licensing, clemency, and public benefits — into private, political, or factional advantage while shifting costs onto weaker actors and reducing the independence of institutions capable of resisting or correcting the flow?

That is the central discriminator for the final article.

The pattern becomes stronger when these converge:

```text

+ material benefit
+ preferential treatment
+ reduced exit power for weaker actors
+ weakened oversight
+ repeated topology across independent domains
```

It becomes weaker when:
- connected actors lose despite proximity;
- policies materially harm Trump-linked financial interests;
- independent oversight remains effective;
- benefits are broadly distributed without preferential routing;
- coercive actions show documented necessity, proportionality, and exhausted alternatives;
- counterexamples demonstrate durable correction after internal dissent.
