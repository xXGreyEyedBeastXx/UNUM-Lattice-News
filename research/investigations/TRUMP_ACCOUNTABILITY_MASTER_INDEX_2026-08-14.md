## Why this file exists

The investigation accumulated many strong individual lanes without one stable numbered list. This file is the address book.

It does **not** replace the deeper source packets. It answers:

```text
What are we investigating about Trump?
What is already adjudicated or documented?
What belongs to Trump personally versus his administration/coalition?
What remains allegation, inference, or open lead?
Where does counterevidence live?
```

Status keys:

- `ADJUDICATED` — established within a court verdict/judgment/plea scope.
- `DOCUMENTED` — directly supported by primary records or strong convergent evidence.
- `SUPPORTED` — substantial evidence, but causal scope remains bounded.
- `ALLEGED` — sourced allegation not independently established.
- `OPEN` — live investigative question.
- `COUNTEREVIDENCE` — evidence that limits an absolute or overbroad claim.
