## Question

Did the Israeli deterrence/containment narrative merely misread Hamas, or did its persistence against accumulating contrary evidence become operationally causal by shaping protection, force allocation, event approval, warning thresholds and civilian exposure before October 7?

## Method

This investigation does not begin from the later official explanation. It begins from the clue field and asks what moved.

```text
CLUE
-> WHO/WHAT RECEIVED IT
-> HOW IT WAS CLASSIFIED
-> WHAT DECISION FOLLOWED
-> WHO RECEIVED PROTECTION / CAPACITY
-> WHO ABSORBED EXPOSURE
-> WHETHER THE MODEL UPDATED
-> WHETHER CONTRADICTIONS ACCUMULATED
```

Official narratives are then added as evidence surfaces, not sovereign frames.

## Why this matters for the 'coincidence' question

These clues should not be treated as independent coincidences when they are joined by shared timing, command structures, force-allocation decisions, threat assessment and protection consequences.

Correct lock:

`COMMON_CAUSAL_SYSTEM != RANDOMLY_INDEPENDENT_EVENTS`

But:

`NON_RANDOM_CAUSAL_RELATION != PROVEN DELIBERATE SETUP`.

The supported finding is that the same governing security model and priority system joined these events into one causal ecology.

**Not established.**

## Locks

- Official reassurance is evidence of a model, not proof the model was reasonable.
- Repeated contrary clues can make persistence itself evidentiary.
- Institutional knowledge must still be resolved to actor-specific knowledge for individual attribution.
- Resource allocation is behavior and therefore evidence.
- Omission/non-action can be evidence when duty, warning and capacity are established.
- A politically consequential causal structure does not require a secret conspiracy.
- A secret-plan hypothesis must not be used to erase already-supported structural responsibility.
- Failure to establish deliberate setup does not restore the unforeseeable-surprise narrative.
