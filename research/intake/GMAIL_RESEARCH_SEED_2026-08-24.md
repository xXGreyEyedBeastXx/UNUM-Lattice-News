## Intake rule

Mailbox material is a discovery surface, not a proof container.

```text
email / newsletter / old correspondence
-> lead or source pointer
-> recover underlying source where possible
-> identify lineage
-> independently corroborate
-> establish chronology / seat / authority / relation
-> test alternatives
-> promote only the surviving bounded claim
```

Repeated reporting inherited from one source is one evidentiary lineage until independently supported.

## Historical-language recovery rule

Older strongly worded correspondence may contain compressed moral or political labels created before the present Lattice vocabulary existed.

Do not inherit those labels as current findings merely because they appear in historical mail. Preserve them as provenance and reconstruct the underlying observations, relations, chronology, harms, notice, authority paths, and alternatives.

```text
old compressed label
-> recover observations / facial facts
-> recover actor and authority seat
-> recover mechanism and chronology
-> recover notice / access / response
-> test competing explanations
-> restate strongest current supported claim
```

A historical claim may survive, narrow, change type, or fail. Revisability does not require historical amnesia.

## Presentation versus care discriminator — candidate research lens

This is a candidate analytical distinction, not a scored moral field.

```text
NICENESS
= interpersonal presentation, civility, charm, low overt aggression,
  politeness, or socially pleasant behavior

KINDNESS
= conduct that protects, equips, respects, repairs, or increases
  another person's practical ability to flourish

CARE_COMPETENCE
= demonstrated ability to recognize needs, teach boundaries and
  self-regulation, preserve agency, provide appropriate protection,
  support help-seeking, and repair harm without merely controlling,
  soothing, neglecting, or performing concern
```

Do not use `nice` as exculpatory evidence for unrelated harmful conduct.
Do not use claimed kindness as evidence without conduct and consequence.
Do not convert one beneficial interaction into transferable moral credit.

## Adversarial independence lock

Two harmful actors opposing one another does not create moral credit for either side and does not establish investigator alignment with either one.

```text
A opposes B
!= A is good
!= B is good
!= investigator supports A
!= investigator supports B
```

`enemy of my enemy` is not an evidence function.
