## Core separation

The Lattice must not allow a change in legal protection, institutional power, immunity, enforcement posture, prosecutorial willingness, or access to evidence to rewrite the underlying event.

```text
conduct
!= constitutional legality
!= statutory legality
!= criminal-law fit
!= immunity
!= enforceability
!= political protection
!= moral or policy evaluation
```

Tiny lock:

> **Impunity is not innocence. Immunity is not legality. Authority is not constitutionality. Reduced enforceability does not rewrite the underlying event.**

The inverse lock is equally important:

> **Unconstitutional, abusive, cruel, corrupt, or self-dealing conduct is not automatically a proved criminal offense. Criminal classification requires the elements of an applicable offense and the required mental state.**

## 2. Terrorism and extremism labels applied beyond proved conduct

The administration has repeatedly used domestic-terror or extremist framing around antifa and anti-ICE protest activity.

AP's review of 166 protest-related federal cases found many serious charges reduced or dismissed and all five defendants in the reviewed set who went to trial acquitted. Separate Reuters reporting has documented official accounts of violent immigration-enforcement encounters later contradicted by video, records, or other evidence.

This does not mean every antifa-affiliated or anti-ICE actor is nonviolent.

It does mean:

```text
official terror / aggressor classifier
!= independent proof of terrorism
```

and that classifier reliability must be assessed case by case.

## 3. Investigation impairment around deaths and shootings

Federal control of evidence surrounding immigration-enforcement shootings has created a distinct accountability problem.

Minnesota prosecutors said federal authorities withheld evidence concerning the killings of Renee Good and Alex Pretti and another shooting for roughly six months before turning over a large evidentiary record after disputes and litigation.

ICE also ended public reporting of deaths occurring within 30 days after release from detention, reducing observability of mortality potentially connected to conditions in custody.

These facts support:

```text

INVESTIGATION_ACCESS_CONSTRAINT
TRANSPARENCY_REDUCTION
```

They do **not** automatically establish criminal obstruction by the President or every official involved. That requires identifying the decision makers, legal duties, intent, and applicable obstruction statutes.

Reuters found extremely large Trump-family crypto income and profit during the presidency. The Washington Post separately traced investment exposure by Donald Trump Jr. and Eric Trump to defense and technology companies receiving or seeking substantial federal business.

The correct research chain is:

```text
private financial position
-> government information / policy access
```

A large profit or a policy overlap does not itself prove insider trading.

But neither does a gap in current criminal law make the underlying self-dealing or conflict fictitious.

## 5. Iran war: initiation, factual predicate, and legal authority

On February 28, 2026, the United States and Israel launched large-scale attacks on Iran.

The United States subsequently invoked Article 51 self-defense at the United Nations. The UN Secretary-General condemned the U.S.-Israeli strikes and emphasized the UN Charter's prohibition on the use of force against the territorial integrity or political independence of a state.

Reuters later documented substantial changes in the administration's stated reasons, objectives, and expected duration of the war. Public fact-checking found that at least some factual predicates advanced by President Trump — including claims concerning reconstruction of damaged enrichment capacity and the near-term missile threat to the United States — lacked publicly demonstrated support or were disputed by relevant experts.

That produces two separate investigations.

### Factual-pretext lane

```text
what Trump and officials said before / at attack
-> what intelligence and IAEA evidence actually showed at that time
-> what officials knew
-> what was omitted or contradicted
-> whether any false statement was material and knowing
```

Changing rationales after hostilities began are evidence about planning and projection, but do not alone prove knowing deception.

### War-authority lane

International:

```text
Article 2(4)
-> Article 51 predicate
-> armed attack / imminence theory if invoked
-> necessity
-> proportionality
-> collective self-defense if invoked
-> Security Council authority or absence
```

Domestic U.S.:

```text
Article I congressional war power
-> claimed Article II authority
-> War Powers Resolution
-> congressional votes / directives
-> duration and re-initiation of hostilities
```

`WAR_OF_AGGRESSION` is therefore a **serious candidate legal classification requiring element-level review**, not a rhetorical label and not something that should be accepted or rejected simply because one belligerent says so.

## 6. The accountability principle

The recurring pattern to test is:

```text
controversial or harmful conduct
-> institutional protection / immunity / personnel loyalty / evidence control
-> reduced probability of investigation or sanction
-> public claim that absence of sanction proves legitimacy
```

The last arrow is invalid.

```text
no prosecution
!= no offense
no injunction
!= constitutional
immunity
!= innocence
political control of enforcement
!= factual exoneration
```

Likewise:

```text
serious accusation
!= proved criminal offense
```

The Lattice should preserve both sides of that discipline.
