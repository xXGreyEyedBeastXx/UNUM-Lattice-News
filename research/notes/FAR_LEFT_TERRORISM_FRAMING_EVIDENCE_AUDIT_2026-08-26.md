## Why this correction exists

The August 26 public-power field update correctly typed the Treasury action as an **observed designation** and the “far-left terrorism” characterization as a **stated government attribution / disputed legal-political framing**. It also explicitly refused to generalize the designations to anti-fascism, Palestine advocacy, protest, or left-wing politics as classes.

That separation should be made even harder to lose.

```text
observed use of counterterrorism authority
!= independently established terrorist identity

government designation
!= neutral threat baseline

government press-release label
!= incident-level proof

existence of some left-wing political violence
!= symmetry of scale, lethality, organization, state tolerance, or historical trajectory
```

The phrase **“far-left terrorism”** should therefore remain attributed to the administration unless a specific incident, organization, and statutory/analytical terrorism predicate are independently established.

## 1. Independent U.S. threat baseline does not support simple left/right symmetry

### CSIS 2025 data

Independent CSIS analysis published September 25–26, 2025 found:

- left-wing terrorist attacks and plots had risen from **very low levels**;
- through July 4, 2025, CSIS counted five left-wing attacks or plots;
- 2025 was the first year in more than three decades in its dataset in which left-wing incidents outnumbered far-right incidents;
- that crossover was driven partly by an unusual decline in right-wing incidents;
- left-wing attack lethality remained **very low**;
- historically, right-wing and jihadist violence remained substantially more lethal and more significant than recent left-wing violence.

Sources:
- CSIS, *Left-Wing Terrorism and Political Violence in the United States: What the Data Tells Us*, 2025-09-25: https://www.csis.org/analysis/left-wing-terrorism-and-political-violence-united-states-what-data-tells-us
- CSIS, *Ideological Trends in U.S. Terrorism*, 2025-09-26: https://www.csis.org/analysis/ideological-trends-us-terrorism

### Older federal baseline

GAO and DHS records preserve the longer-running domestic-terrorism problem and the particularly lethal role of violent white-supremacist and anti-government movements in prior periods. Federal reporting has repeatedly described domestic violent extremism as a major or persistent terrorism threat, while earlier DHS material identified violent white supremacists as especially persistent and lethal.

Sources:
- GAO-23-104720, *Domestic Terrorism: Further Actions Needed to Strengthen FBI and DHS Collaboration to Counter Threats*: https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-23-104720
- GAO, *The Rising Threat of Domestic Terrorism in the U.S. and Federal Efforts to Combat It*: https://www.gao.gov/blog/rising-threat-domestic-terrorism-u.s.-and-federal-efforts-combat-it
- DHS, *Homeland Threat Assessment 2025*: https://www.dhs.gov/sites/default/files/2024-10/24_0930_ia_24-320-ia-publication-2025-hta-final-30sep24-508.pdf

### Correct comparative posture

The independent record therefore does **not** support either of these shortcuts:

```text
“there is literally no left-wing terrorism”
```

or

```text
“left-wing and right-wing terrorism are equivalent threats”
```

A stronger evidence-bounded statement is:

> Recent datasets identify a small but real rise in U.S. left-wing attacks/plots from a very low baseline, while the longer historical record shows far-right political violence as substantially more lethal and, across major periods, more consequential. A one-year incident-count crossover does not erase that historical asymmetry.

## 2. January 6 is material comparative evidence, not background decoration

The January 6, 2021 Capitol attack is a concrete case of mass right-wing political violence directed at the constitutional transfer/certification process.

GAO found that federal agencies had identified credible threats before the attack but did not consistently process or share critical threat information. Reuters and federal court records document extensive violence against police and organized extremist participation.

Sources:
- GAO-23-106625, *Capitol Attack: Federal Agencies Identified Some Threats, but Did Not Fully Process and Share Information Prior to January 6, 2021*: https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-23-106625
- Reuters, 2025-01-06, reporting more than 140 police officers injured and serious convictions arising from the attack: https://www.reuters.com/world/us/anniversary-us-capitol-attack-proud-boys-ex-leader-asks-trump-pardon-2025-01-06/
- DOJ clemency ledger confirming the January 20, 2025 pardons/commutations for January 6 offenses: https://www.justice.gov/pardon/clemency-grants-president-donald-j-trump-2025-present

### Edward Jacob “Jake” Lang

Lang is especially useful as an incident-level discriminator because the evidence is not merely ideological labeling.

A D.C. Circuit order summarized the government evidence as showing that Lang repeatedly pushed, punched, and kicked police officers; slammed a door against an officer’s head; struck officers with a stolen riot shield and a metal baseball bat; later advocated taking up arms against the government; and attempted to organize militias for future violence.

Sources:
- D.C. Circuit, *United States v. Edward Jacob Lang*, 2022-01-12: https://media.cadc.uscourts.gov/orders/docs/2022/01/21-3066LDSJ.pdf
- DOJ indictment: https://www.justice.gov/d9/langindictment.pdf

He was later among January 6 defendants pardoned after President Trump returned to office. That clemency is a distinct state action; it does not retroactively erase the documented conduct.

Operational lock:

```text
pardon != factual erasure
clemency != claim-defeating evidence against the underlying documented act
```

## 3. Treasury’s August 26 action must be decomposed organization by organization

Treasury’s press release calls Autistici/Inventati, Palestine Action, and Masar Badil part of a “violent far-left terrorist” threat and invokes E.O. 13224.

Source:
- U.S. Treasury, 2026-08-26: https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0616/

The **designation and sanctions are observed state actions**. Treasury’s factual assertions about each organization remain government claims unless independently corroborated at the incident/relationship level.

### Palestine Action

Treasury states that Palestine Action supported acts involving injuries, break-ins at defense and military facilities, intimidation/coercion, and substantial property damage. The UK proscribed the organization in 2025, but that proscription has been legally challenged and remains politically and legally contested.

Required research separation:

```text
specific break-in / assault / damage event
-> establish actor, conduct, intent, injury, target, evidence

specific terrorism-law predicate
-> establish statutory fit and adjudicative status

group ideology / Palestine advocacy
-> does not supply missing conduct evidence
```

Do not infer:

```text
Palestine advocacy -> terrorism
anti-Zionism -> terrorism
property damage -> automatic terrorism under every jurisdiction
UK proscription -> automatic independent U.S. factual proof
```

### Autistici/Inventati

Treasury alleges technological/material support relationships with violent organizations and designated groups. Independent verification should trace the actual services, recipient identities, knowledge/notice, legal status, and whether ordinary privacy/infrastructure services are being converted into material-support attribution by association.

### Masar Badil

Treasury’s claim is substantially a relationship/control claim involving Samidoun/PFLP-linked structures. This should be tested as a typed organizational and financial relation rather than generalized through a left/right ideological label.

## 4. Correct interpretation of the August 26 state transition

The strongest currently supported state transition is:

```text
prior:
  existing U.S. counterterrorism authority
  + administration rhetoric identifying “far-left terrorism” as a priority

operation:
  Treasury applies E.O. 13224 sanctions/designations to named organizations and people

later:
  real financial / association / service-provider constraints expand
  while the factual basis and ideological scope of the “far-left terrorism” frame remain claim-specific and contestable
```

This is a **security-capacity and classification expansion** even if every designation later proves legally valid. The investigative question is not whether political violence should be immunized; it is whether the state keeps conduct, organization, association, ideology, and protected political activity correctly separated.

## 5. Anti-false-equivalence lock

Use the same evidentiary burden across ideological directions, but do not force symmetric conclusions.

```text
same method != same result
same label != same scale
one left-wing attack != right/left equivalence
one right-wing attack != proof every right-wing actor is violent
state designation != neutral measurement
absence of designation != absence of violence
```

For comparative threat work, track at minimum:

```text
incident count
completed attacks vs disrupted plots
fatalities
injuries
organized-network involvement
state/political sponsorship or tolerance where evidenced
target class
capacity
persistence over time
legal/adjudicative status
clemency / normalization / institutional response
```

A political movement receiving more state counterterrorism attention is not evidence that it produces more terrorism.
