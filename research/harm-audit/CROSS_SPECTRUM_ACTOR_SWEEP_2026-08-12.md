## Purpose

Begin running highly controversial public figures through the Harm Hierarchy Legibility Adversarial Audit without treating public controversy itself as evidence of harm.

This sweep intentionally includes figures liked and disliked across ideological factions. The task is not to make each person look equally bad. It is to identify:

```text
confirmed harm
confirmed concentrated-power contribution
confirmed harmful intent / attempted harm
catastrophic-risk contribution
pending allegations
path-forward investigations
debunked claims only after full explanation
```

A low harm finding is a valid result.
