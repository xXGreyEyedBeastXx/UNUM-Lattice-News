**Typed digestion:**
- source regime: armed conflict + maritime chokepoint + global energy system
- active difference: negotiated de-escalation versus renewed coercion and shipping disruption
- carrier/field: naval/missile capability, commercial shipping, insurance, oil inventories, diplomatic channels
- constraints: military leverage, bargaining demands, navigational insecurity, finite inventories, civilian exposure
- transformation: conflict/deadlock -> reduced passage -> supply risk/prices -> downstream household and industrial costs
- persistence/closure: no durable closure; earlier reopening assumptions have proven reversible
- projection loss risk: price-only coverage can erase civilian casualties and the coercive power created by chokepoint control; diplomatic claims can also overstate actual implementation
- surviving trace: vessel traffic, attacks, negotiated texts, prices/inventories, civilian casualty records
- next questions: what concrete terms block reopening; who bears the largest food/fuel/transport burden; which claims about ceasefire status are independently verifiable?

## 5. Truth/correction watch: viral “Earth loses gravity for seven seconds” claim fails basic source and physics checks

- Contemporary reporting says NASA explicitly rejected the rumor; no credible gravitational-wave observation or physical mechanism supports it.

**Typed digestion:**
- source regime: viral misinformation + basic gravitational physics + astronomical event context
- active difference: fabricated authority claim versus checkable institutional record and physical mechanism
- carrier/field: social media, AI-generated/replicated content, eclipse attention
- constraints: sensational incentives, citation laundering, scientific illiteracy, event-date coincidence
- transformation: invented “leak” -> repetition -> apparent authority -> correction by provenance/mechanism check
- persistence/closure: factual claim is closed as false absent extraordinary new evidence
- projection loss risk: debunking only the absurd endpoint can miss the transmission mechanism—fake document provenance plus attachment to a real celestial event
- surviving trace: absence from NASA records, original viral posts, real eclipse documentation
- next questions: which amplification networks drove renewed circulation and how effectively does source-first correction reduce belief?
