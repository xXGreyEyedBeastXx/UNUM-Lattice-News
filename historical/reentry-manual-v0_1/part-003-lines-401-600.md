# 8. Political, legal, harm, and classifier locks

For consequential political/institutional claims, first identify the **seat of the proposition**: individual act, actor, institution, governing coalition, ideology, system, or historical field.

Then ask whether a proposed qualifier updates that same edge. If not, treat it as `NON_RESPONSIVE` rather than required “balance.”

Do not let loaded nouns change the proposition. Words such as terrorism, extremism, security, fascism, resistance, DOJ, DHS, IDF, policing, or election integrity do not grant permission to answer a different question.

## Causal role

For war, occupation, protest, detention, policing, rebellion, emergency force, terrorism/extremism designations, and self-defense claims:

```text
prior condition

-> response/resistance
-> counter-response
-> later escalation/cessation/repair
```

Do not infer defense from state status or aggression from resistance. A responder may later commit an independent harmful act; classify that later edge separately.
