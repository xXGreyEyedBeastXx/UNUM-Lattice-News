# Harm Audit Proof Dossier Standard v0.1

**Status:** active working publication standard  
**Purpose:** make consequential Harm Mesh articles independently reconstructable by a skeptical reader.

## 1. Core requirement

A Harm Audit article is not publication-ready merely because it has many citations or a coherent conclusion.

It is ready when the consequential causal chain is exposed far enough that a critic must identify **which source, identity, relation, transformation, attribution, counterfactual, or endpoint fails**.

```text
claim
-> evidence object
-> actor / entity identity
-> operative relation
-> material transformation
-> harm / constraint
-> gain / capacity
-> terminal or reseated beneficiary
-> influence / reproduction
-> redress
```

Every material arrow must be either:

1. supported by a recoverable evidence anchor;
2. explicitly typed as an inference with its reasoning exposed; or
3. marked unresolved with a concrete path forward.

The goal is not to make disagreement impossible. **The goal is to make disagreement specific.**

---

## 2. Reader-facing dossier structure

Each dense harm-audit article should contain or link to the following proof surfaces.

### A. Scoped claim

State the strongest claim actually supported by the evidence, not the broadest slogan that could describe it.

Include:
- actor or system;
- time window;
- jurisdiction;
- mechanism;
- affected population or ecology;
- evidentiary posture;
- investigation state.

### B. Chain diagram

Render the main mechanism in plain language before interpretation.

Example:

```text
taxpayer funds
-> public agency
-> private contractor
-> service / enforcement capacity
-> contractor revenue
-> corporate cash flow
-> dividends / repurchases / retained capacity
```

A diagram is a navigation surface, not evidence by itself.

### C. Edge ledger

For every material crossing, expose a ledger with at least:

```text
edge_id
from
relation / transformation
to
claim posture
investigation state
source(s)
source lineage
scope / time window
counterevidence or alternative
what would weaken or falsify the edge
```

Do not hide an inferential jump inside prose between two well-cited endpoints.

### D. Money / resource ledger

Where value moves, distinguish:

```text
appropriation
obligation
outlay / disbursement
loan
loan guarantee
insurance / risk exposure
tax expenditure
contract revenue
gross revenue
operating income
net income
cash flow
dividend
share repurchase
debt service
asset appreciation
public or institutional capacity
```

Do not convert one accounting category into another merely because they appear in the same circuit.

### E. Identity and ownership lineage

Do not stop at the first familiar name.

Trace where relevant:

```text
brand
-> legal entity
-> subsidiary / operating company
-> parent
-> holding company
-> fund / trust / investment vehicle
-> beneficial or voting-control seat
```

Preserve predecessor/successor, DBA, merger, spinoff, acquisition, management-company, REIT, creditor, and related-party relations where material.

### F. Harm and constraint ledger

Separate:
- physical or lethal harm;
- confinement / coercion;
- material deprivation;
- labor / wage loss;
- rights / agency / refusal / exit harm;
- land / sovereignty / displacement;
- ecological burden;
- democratic / epistemic harm;
- power concentration;
- catastrophic risk.

Name who bears each consequence and whether it is observed, inferred, potential, disputed, or unresolved.

### G. Beneficiary ledger

A beneficiary is not merely whoever appears near the money.

Trace:

```text
immediate recipient
-> costs / obligations
-> retained margin or capacity
-> parent / creditor / owner
-> dividend / buyback / fee / interest / appreciation / political or coercive capacity
```

If the terminal beneficiary cannot yet be resolved, say so.

### H. Influence ledger

Where a beneficiary also lobbies, donates, litigates, comments on rules, participates in trade groups, or otherwise seeks policy influence, preserve the overlap **without promoting it into causation**.

```text
benefits from policy
+
lobbies on policy
=
documented incentive + attempted influence

NOT automatically:
policy was bought / authored / controlled by beneficiary
```

Stronger claims require stronger evidence: drafting records, communications, quid-pro-quo evidence, coordinated strategy, decisive access, or another independently supported causal crossing.

### I. Redress timeline

Show what peaceful correction mechanisms existed and what happened to them:
- internal appeal;
- regulator;
- court;
- union / collective bargaining;
- boycott / strike / protest;
- election / administrative review;
- inspector / auditor / whistleblower;
- financial assistance;
- rulemaking or statutory correction;
- settlement or consent decree.

Distinguish:

```text
FUNCTIONING
FUNCTIONING_WITH_LIMITS
DELAYED
BYPASSED
DEFUNDED
INTIMIDATED
QUORUM_IMPAIRED
CAPTURED
INACCESSIBLE
RESTORED
```

Restoration does not erase a prior failure interval.

### J. Strongest countercase

Before publication, reconstruct the strongest evidence that could make the scoped claim wrong, narrower, less causal, less severe, or differently attributed.

Counterevidence is not an unrelated positive biography.

Examples:
- legitimate delivered public value;
- scale economies;
- alternative causal drivers;
- independent market forces;
- protective regulation;
- actor-specific nonparticipation;
- chronology that defeats a proposed causal sequence;
- evidence that gain was passed through to workers or consumers;
- functioning redress that materially repaired the harm.

### K. What would change the assessment

End the evidence section with explicit re-entry conditions.

```text
We would narrow or reverse this conclusion if...
```

Name the record, measurement, counterfactual, or contradictory evidence that would change the claim.

## 3. Hostile-reader test

Before publication, give the article the strongest adversarial reading practical.

Ask:

1. Can a critic identify a missing transformation between two cited facts?
2. Did we stop at a brand, subsidiary, agency, or accounting category that is not the terminal seat?
3. Did we convert lobbying into policy control without evidence?
4. Did we convert public revenue into profit or shareholder return dollar-for-dollar?
5. Did we convert concentration into collusion?
6. Did we convert formal legality into legitimacy or voluntariness?
7. Did we convert a monitor's extremist classification into criminal liability?
8. Did we label an individual as a group member from mere proximity or event attendance?
9. Did we use repeated reporting descended from one source as independent corroboration?
10. Did we hide the strongest claim-specific counterevidence?
11. Is a current legal or institutional status actually current?
12. What exact edge would a good-faith critic have to defeat to change the conclusion?

If the answer to 1-10 reveals a real defect, return the article to investigation.

---

## 4. Evidence density

Dense does not mean unreadable.

Use three layers:

```text
ARTICLE
  readable narrative and key chain

PROOF DOSSIER
  edge ledger, money flow, ownership, countercase, redress

SOURCE RECORDS
  recoverable primary documents and lineage
```

The article should not demand that the reader accept the entire worldview in order to verify one mechanism.

A skeptical reader should be able to enter at any edge, inspect the evidence, and move outward.

---

## 5. Extremism and adversarial audiences

When reporting on nationalist, white-nationalist, supremacist, neo-Nazi, Ku Klux Klan, Proud Boys, White Lives Matter, or other extremist activity, use the narrowest evidence-supported identity.

```text
nationalist != white nationalist
white nationalist != KKK
white supremacist != neo-Nazi automatically
participant != member
member != leader
membership != responsibility for every act
SPLC / ADL classification != criminal adjudication
hate-group designation by a monitor != government terrorist designation
```

This precision is not a concession to extremist ideology. It protects the evidentiary chain from an avoidable terminology failure.

Do not dehumanize an actor. Do not reduce evidentiary burdens because an actor holds hateful views. Do not allow hateful ideology to shield evidenced conduct from scrutiny either.

---

## 6. Publication lock

A Harm Audit article may make a forceful moral argument after the material mechanism is visible.

It must never require the reader to trust the moral label in order to see the evidence.

```text
mechanism first
-> evidence chain
-> harm and beneficiary
-> countercase
-> redress
-> moral / political interpretation
```

The strongest public proof is not a pile of citations.

**It is a chain whose arrows can be inspected.**
