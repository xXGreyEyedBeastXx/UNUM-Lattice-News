# Regime Context and Causal-Role Gate v0.1

**Status:** ACTIVE / CROSS-SURFACE ORIENTATION RULE  
**Introduced:** 2026-08-27  
**Purpose:** prevent isolated-event analysis from repeatedly resetting the political baseline, while preventing regime labels from substituting for event-level evidence.

## 1. Why this gate exists

Public events do not occur in a blank institutional field.

A sequence of verified changes to elections, courts, civil service, policing, military authority, prosecution, media, education, unions, protest, surveillance, minority rights, emergency power, oversight, patronage, or political violence may alter the regime context in which later events occur.

If every story starts from an assumed ordinary-democratic baseline, persistent authoritarian change can be laundered into a collection of unrelated exceptions.

If every story starts from a totalizing regime label, the label can become an accusation engine that bypasses claim-specific evidence.

Both failures are prohibited.

```text
persistent verified context != blank slate
regime label != proof of every local claim
```

## 2. Two simultaneous scales

Maintain both:

```text
LOCAL EVENT SCALE
  What happened here?
  Who acted first in the bounded causal sequence?
  What changed?
  What evidence bears on each edge?

REGIME / FIELD SCALE
  What governing constraints, capacities, protections, coercive pathways,
  ideological hierarchies, accountability systems, and opposition conditions
  were already active before this event?
```

A local event may update the regime field. The regime field may supply relevant prior condition for a local event. Neither may silently replace the other.

## 3. Authoritarian and fascist classification discipline

`authoritarian`, `fascistic`, and `fascist` are analytical classifications, not team labels.

Do not require the system to remain at `NOT_ASSESSED` merely because scholars or sources disagree over the final noun. Preserve the evidenced dimensions separately.

Recommended simultaneous states:

```text
democratic_constraint_state:
  FUNCTIONING | STRAINED | ERODING | SEVERELY_ERODED | PARTLY_CAPTURED | CAPTURED | UNKNOWN

authoritarian_formation:
  UNSUPPORTED | INDICATORS_PRESENT | SUPPORTED | STRONGLY_SUPPORTED | DISPUTED

fascist_pattern:
  UNSUPPORTED | INDICATORS_PRESENT | SUPPORTED | STRONGLY_SUPPORTED | DISPUTED

regime_classification:
  OPEN | MIXED | AUTHORITARIAN_SUPPORTED | FASCIST_CLASSIFICATION_SUPPORTED | DISPUTED | UNKNOWN
```

A final `FASCIST_CLASSIFICATION_SUPPORTED` state should identify the definition or scholarly model being applied and the evidence for each required dimension. A disagreement over the final label must not erase independently established authoritarian or fascistic mechanisms.

## 5. Resistance is not exculpation

Countervailing institutions and successful resistance must be recorded as real surviving constraints.

They do not automatically reset the regime field.

```text
court blocks an attempted abuse
-> CONSTRAINT_RESISTANCE + REALIZED_OUTCOME
!= proof the attempt did not occur
!= proof the broader authoritarian direction is absent

free election occurs
-> evidence that an electoral mechanism survived for that scope/time
!= automatic proof all electoral or governing institutions are fully democratic

journalist / university / union successfully resists pressure
-> surviving resistance
!= proof pressure was not exerted
```

A system can contain substantial democratic resistance while authoritarian formation is underway. The state must preserve both pressures.

## 6. Aggression / defense / resistance gate

Whenever an event involves force, occupation, policing, protest, rebellion, repression, terrorism/extremism designation, insurgency, war, domestic military deployment, detention, border enforcement, or coercive emergency power, the `AGGRESSION_DEFENSE_RESISTANCE_PASSPORT_v0_1` is mandatory before assigning causal role.

Reconstruct:

```text
prior condition
-> initiating coercion / aggression
-> response / resistance
-> counter-response
-> later escalation / cessation / repair
```

Do not infer:

```text
state actor -> defender
nonstate actor -> aggressor
recognized government -> lawful force
resistance -> initiating violence
terrorism/extremism label -> causal role
weaker/oppressed actor -> every tactic defensive
initial defense -> unlimited later force
```

Self-defense and resistance are act/edge classifications, not permanent identities. A responding actor may commit a later independent aggression; an initiating aggressor may later face unlawful retaliation. Classify each crossing separately.

## 7. Prior-condition carry-forward

A verified regime context is a reusable `S0` component for later news transitions.

Do not force each story to re-prove the entire preceding political field. Instead:

```text
regime_context_record
-> cited as prior condition
-> local event supplies new evidence/delta
-> regime context updated only where the event bears
```

This prevents two opposite errors:

```text
context amnesia
  every abuse appears unprecedented and isolated

context determinism
  every action is treated as proven by the regime label
```

## 8. Evidence jurisdiction and anti-laundering locks

Common laundering failures:

```text
successful resistance -> "there was no authoritarian attempt"
official legality -> "there was no coercive mechanism"
continued election -> "democratic erosion is impossible"
public condemnation -> "contradictory conduct disappears"
security label -> "state is defender"
terror label -> "target initiated aggression"
institutional prestige -> "institutional account is independently true"
uncertain motive -> "documented mechanism becomes uncertain"
complexity -> "no bounded conclusion can be reached"
```

Treat each as an evidence-jurisdiction question, not as automatic laundering. The transformation must be shown.

## 10. Minimum review questions

Before publishing or promoting a consequential political-power story, ask:

1. What local event is established?
2. What prior political/institutional condition is materially relevant?
3. Is there an active regime-context record that should be cited?
4. Does this event update a regime-context dimension, or merely occur within it?
5. If force/resistance is involved, where does the causal chronology actually begin?
6. Is the first visible violent act also the initiating coercive act?
7. What surviving resistance or accountability mechanism operated?
8. Is successful resistance being misused to erase the attempted direction?
9. Is an institutional/rhetorical label being used as causal proof?
10. What evidence would narrow, defeat, or reroute the exact local or regime-level claim?

## Tiny lock

> Do not reset the field to normal every morning. Do not let the field predetermine every edge either. Carry forward what is established. Restore the first relevant coercion. Preserve resistance as resistance. Let every claim keep its own evidence.
