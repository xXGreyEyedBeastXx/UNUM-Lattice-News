# Operational Narrative as Evidence v0.1

Status: ACTIVE METHODOLOGY / EXTENSIBLE EVIDENCE GRAMMAR
Date: 2026-08-17

## Purpose

Preserve a distinction between a narrative that merely describes events and a narrative/model that becomes **operational** because institutions allocate protection, force, money, attention, permissions, warnings, access, and accountability as though that model were true.

A reassuring official story is not important only because it may be accurate or inaccurate. When the same story persists despite material contrary evidence and continues to organize real decisions, the **persistence and operational consequences of the narrative become clues in their own right**.

## Root lock

```text
FALSE_OR_INCOMPLETE_NARRATIVE != SETUP
NARRATIVE_PERSISTENCE != PROVEN_DECEPTION
MODEL_PROTECTIVE_FILTERING != ABSENCE_OF INFORMATION
OPERATIONALIZED_MODEL != MERE RHETORIC
REALITY_CONTRADICTED_MODEL + CONSEQUENTIAL_DECISIONS = FIRST_CLASS_CLUE
SETUP requires an additional intended-outcome / instrumental-purpose edge
```

## New evidence types

### `REALITY_CONTRADICTED_MODEL`

A governing model, doctrine, or public/internal explanatory frame materially conflicts with observed facts available in the relevant period.

Examples of clue forms:
- attack plan exists while threat is treated as implausible;
- visible capability changes while capability is treated as static;
- repeated violence while a theater is described as effectively calm;
- resource shortages while a vulnerable event is treated as adequately protectable.

This type does **not** by itself establish that decision-makers knew the model was false.

### `NARRATIVE_PERSISTENCE_AGAINST_CONTRARY_EVIDENCE`

The same model survives multiple independent contradictory clues without proportionate updating.

Required trace:
- identify the model;
- identify contrary clues and dates;
- identify who or what organizational layer received them when recoverable;
- identify whether the model changed;
- preserve any reasons offered for non-update.

### `MODEL_PROTECTIVE_FILTERING`

Contrary evidence is repeatedly interpreted, downgraded, compartmentalized, normalized, or excluded in ways that preserve the governing model.

This is stronger than simple error because the pattern occurs across multiple clues or repeated opportunities to update. It still does not automatically prove deceptive intent.

### `NARRATIVE_ENABLED_RISK_NORMALIZATION`

A reassuring or simplifying model makes an objectively serious exposure appear ordinary, acceptable, or administratively manageable, reducing the likelihood of protective action.

### `OPERATIONALIZED_NARRATIVE`

A narrative/model has observable downstream effects on:
- troop or police deployment;
- protective coverage;
- event approval;
- warning thresholds;
- evacuation decisions;
- border/access policy;
- budget or staffing;
- enforcement priorities;
- accountability response.

Once a model changes material allocation, it must be investigated as part of the causal chain rather than treated as disposable rhetoric.

### `OPERATIONAL_PRETEXT`

Use only where evidence supports that a stated model or justification was knowingly used to enable or legitimate conduct while materially contrary facts were understood by relevant decision-makers.

### `SETUP_HYPOTHESIS`

Use only as a hypothesis where the clue field suggests a condition may have been deliberately created or preserved to enable a desired downstream event.

Do not promote to finding without evidence of:
1. relevant knowledge;
2. capacity to alter the condition;
3. conscious choice to preserve/create the condition;
4. a desired or instrumentally useful downstream outcome linked to that choice.

## Clue-first test

When a later official explanation appears, ask first:

> If the institution had issued no explanation at all, what causal model would we infer from the behavior, timing, allocation, omissions, warnings, contradictions, and effects?

Then add the official account and ask:

- What does it explain?
- What does it fail to explain?
- What contrary evidence does it omit or downgrade?
- Did the same frame exist before the outcome?
- Did decisions materially follow from the frame?
- Did the frame persist after disconfirming evidence accumulated?
- Who benefited from the allocation or policy produced by the frame?
- Who absorbed the risk or harm?
- Is there evidence the false/incomplete frame was knowingly preserved for an objective?

## Evidence ladder

```text
MISTAKE / MISREAD
-> REALITY_CONTRADICTED_MODEL
-> NARRATIVE_PERSISTENCE_AGAINST_CONTRARY_EVIDENCE
-> MODEL_PROTECTIVE_FILTERING
-> NARRATIVE_ENABLED_RISK_NORMALIZATION
-> OPERATIONALIZED_NARRATIVE
-> KNOWING PRESERVATION candidate
-> OPERATIONAL_PRETEXT candidate
-> SETUP_HYPOTHESIS
-> DELIBERATE SETUP finding only if intent/benefit edge is evidenced
```

Skipping rungs is evidentiary collapse.

## October 7 / Nova specimen

Current linked investigations establish a clue field in which the Israeli deterrence/containment model persisted while:

- Israeli intelligence possessed strategic and detailed ground-incursion concepts;
- internal personnel warned that Hamas had moved toward practiced capability;
- public Hamas exercises visibly rehearsed border-breach / base-and-community assault tactics;
- the Gaza border experienced recurring rocket/airstrike exchanges and renewed September 2023 fence violence;
- work-permit and crossing restrictions were closed/reopened as if instability remained manageable through calibrated pressure and economic incentives;
- local troop-availability concerns existed when Nova was approved near Gaza;
- Gaza reinforcement capacity was moved toward West Bank/Huwara requirements;
- Huwara simultaneously consumed military capacity in an asymmetric settler-security field;
- overnight Oct. 6-7 intelligence included possible offensive-intent warnings;
- Nova was not adequately integrated into the defense picture and was not evacuated.

The supported mechanism is therefore not merely:

`OFFICIALS SAID HAMAS WAS DETERRED`.

It is:

```text
DETERRENCE / CONTAINMENT MODEL
-> contradictory clues normalized or discounted
-> risk classified below mass-incursion threshold
-> protective posture remains inadequate
-> vulnerable civilian concentration approved / not evacuated
-> material exposure increases
```

Current posture:

- `REALITY_CONTRADICTED_MODEL` — SUPPORTED.
- `NARRATIVE_PERSISTENCE_AGAINST_CONTRARY_EVIDENCE` — SUPPORTED.
- `MODEL_PROTECTIVE_FILTERING` — SUPPORTED.
- `NARRATIVE_ENABLED_RISK_NORMALIZATION` — SUPPORTED.
- `OPERATIONALIZED_NARRATIVE` — SUPPORTED at system level.
- `KNOWING_PRESERVATION_OF_FALSE_MODEL_FOR_POLITICAL_PURPOSE` — UNRESOLVED.
- `OPERATIONAL_PRETEXT` — UNRESOLVED.
- `DELIBERATE_SETUP` — NOT ESTABLISHED.

## Locks

- Clue convergence can justify a stronger hypothesis without a confession.
- Clue convergence does not permit inventing the missing intent edge.
- A model can be sincerely believed and still become causally dangerous.
- Sincere belief does not erase responsibility for repeated failure to update against contrary evidence.
- An operational narrative can produce deadly exposure without the deaths being desired.
- Repeated refusal or failure to update is itself an accountability object.
- Official narrative receives no evidentiary privilege merely because it is official.
- `COINCIDENCE` should not be used as a solvent for events already joined by common command, timing, allocation, or causal mechanisms.
