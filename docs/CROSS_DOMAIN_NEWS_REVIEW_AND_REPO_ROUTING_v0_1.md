# UNUM-Lattice-News — Cross-Domain News Review and Repository Routing v0.1

**Status:** active working routing layer  
**Introduced:** 2026-08-22  
**Purpose:** preserve the reusable review and cross-repository custody rules formerly coupled to the recurring Daily Truth Briefing format.

This document is cadence-neutral. It applies whether an item arrives through manual research, a review-only feed candidate, a focused investigation, a one-time briefing, or another approved intake surface.

## 1. Root separation

Keep distinct:

```text
external evidence
project-native interpretation
specialist technical study
public news/accountability record
```

A cross-domain item may participate in more than one repository without giving each repository the same jurisdiction over the claim.

```text
News owns public evidence / provenance / public-state change.
Specialist repos own domain-specific derivation or study.
Heart Core receives only durable architecture-level reconciliation.
```

## 2. Candidate review

Before routing an item, recover:

- strongest available source;
- date and provenance;
- event time versus publication time where they differ;
- source class;
- exact claim posture;
- materially different corroboration or contradiction where consequences warrant it;
- uncertainty and typed missingness;
- affected participants/ecologies where relevant;
- whether the item represents a new event, new evidence about an old event, a projection change, or no supported material state change.

Use `docs/NEWS_STATE_TRANSITION_MODEL_v0_1.md` when the difference between those states matters.

## 3. Typed digestion pass

A selected item may receive a second, explicitly typed analytical pass without replacing the source's native model.

```text
Known
-> source regime
-> causal role
-> native formalism
-> crossing
-> project seat
-> survivors / losses
-> next questions
```

Useful questions include:

- What difference or change is active?
- What field, institution, medium, substrate, network, or carrier transmits it?
- What constrains passage or action?
- What transformation is actually documented?
- What persists, stabilizes, closes, or fails to close?
- What becomes locally readable from the selected seat?
- What is lost or hidden by the projection, source frame, metric, or reporting surface?
- What trace survives strongly enough for later comparison?
- What consequence, dependency, or new question does the result generate?

### Digestion locks

```text
source != project interpretation
correspondence != identity
project-native interpretation != external evidence
failure to recognize bridge != evidence of absence
contradiction requires seat/scale/clock/jurisdiction/population/regime check
interesting finding != automatic canonical-core promotion
```

## 4. Repository custody

### UNUM-Lattice-News

Owns the evidence/accountability record for:

- public events and developments;
- reporting and source comparisons;
- institutional behavior;
- public scientific/technical developments;
- power, constraint, harm, consequence, beneficiary, and correction relations;
- public-state transitions;
- truth-integrity analysis;
- correction history.

The News record should preserve provenance, claim posture, evidence jurisdiction, uncertainty, affected populations/ecologies, state-change clocks where material, and re-entry.

### UNUM-Physics

Route additionally to `UNUM-Physics` when an item warrants genuine derivation, formal correspondence study, mathematical testing, or recovery comparison with the physics body.

```text
News = evidence/provenance/public-state surface
Physics = technical derivation/study surface
```

Cross-reference; do not duplicate authority.

### UNUM-Medical

Route additionally to `UNUM-Medical` when an item creates a durable medical-study question, treatment-comparison question, biological mechanism study, or case-relevant research surface.

```text
News = public evidence surface
Medical = medical interpretation/study surface
```

Cross-reference; do not silently promote public reporting into clinical fact.

### Other specialist repositories

Use the same custody rule for environmental, human-relations, governance, extremism, translation, or other specialist repositories:

```text
route by causal/study function
preserve the News evidence seat
preserve the specialist domain seat
cross-reference the crossing
```

### Unum_Heart_Core

Do not promote ordinary news findings into the canonical core merely because they are interesting or project-relevant.

Core reconciliation is reserved for findings that materially alter a canonical architecture, formal registry, governance rule, or durable cross-repository relation and survive the relevant review route.

## 5. Automation boundary

Automated discovery may identify and organize candidates. It must not silently convert an external item into a published lattice claim.

Before a candidate becomes a durable source, story, state-transition, node, relation, contribution, spread, or rendition record, the repository review gates still apply.

Automation may prepare review packets only within the boundaries in:

- `EDITORIAL_STANDARD.md`;
- `docs/NEWS_OPERATIONS_v0_2.md`;
- `docs/NEWS_INTAKE_WORKFLOW_v0_1.md`;
- `docs/NEWS_STATE_TRANSITION_MODEL_v0_1.md`.

Consequential claims affecting identifiable people or organizations remain subject to the repository's human-review boundary.

## 6. Correction and re-entry

A routed item is not frozen.

Later evidence may change:

- claim posture;
- confidence;
- causal map;
- state-transition read;
- affected-population assessment;
- project correspondence;
- repository custody;
- whether the item remains worth tracking.

Preserve what changed and why.

```text
source
-> reviewed News record
-> optional typed digestion
-> specialist route when warranted
-> new evidence / correction
-> revised News and/or specialist record
```

## Tiny lock

> Gather widely. Verify narrowly. Preserve the native thing. Separate the evidence seat from the project seat. Route by function, not by excitement. Let correction change the next read.
