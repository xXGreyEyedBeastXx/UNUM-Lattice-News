# Harm Audit Article Readiness — 2026-08-16

This note is a publication-preparation surface, not an article draft.

## Standard

An article is ready to draft when the repository can expose, in reader-verifiable form:

1. the harm or grievance;
2. the material mechanism;
3. the actor or institution controlling the relevant term;
4. the money/resource/power flow;
5. the person or ecology bearing the loss;
6. the terminal or reseated beneficiary;
7. the narrative or legal frame that obscures or legitimizes the arrangement;
8. the functioning or failure of redress;
9. the strongest claim-specific counterevidence;
10. the evidence that would change the assessment.

A strong article should be reconstructable without trusting the author.

## Highest-readiness packages after this pass

### 1. Housing: algorithmic rent and terminal beneficiary

The strongest article is not “all landlords collude.” It is the narrower documented question: when major landlords share competitively sensitive information through common pricing systems, who receives the resulting rent and who is blamed for housing pressure? The RealPage enforcement lineage provides a primary-source spine and multiple defendants/settlements.

### 2. Healthcare: the middleman that owns the pharmacy

The PBM package is unusually strong because the FTC quantified both markups and spread-pricing income while identifying vertical integration and affiliated-pharmacy reimbursement differences. Current settlements supply a redress section instead of leaving the article as a pure accusation.

### 3. Student debt: outsourced gatekeeper, weakened checker

GAO provides the oversight failure and borrower-risk evidence; SEC filings provide the private revenue seat. The strongest frame is administrative distance: the public owns the debt, private companies administer the borrower relationship, and the public agency reduced direct accuracy/call-quality testing after losing staff.

### 4. Worker surveillance: when the boss owns the measurement

GAO supplies a measured positive/negative evidence base rather than an ideological one. This article should distinguish safety-enhancing surveillance from systems that increase pace pressure, injury risk, stress, inaccurate scoring, discipline, or termination. The next fill should resolve vendors and employer case studies.

### 5. Public AI/security contracting: money and capacity move together

Palantir is a clean case for the dual-flow proposition: taxpayer money becomes private revenue while software expands state operational capacity. The article must not infer abusive use from capability alone. Use-case evidence is required before attaching specific human harm.

## Writing posture

Do not lead with labels such as fascism, communism, corporate welfare, corruption, or slavery when the mechanism can be shown first. Let the evidence create the category pressure.

Preferred sequence:

```text
Here is what happened.
Here is who paid.
Here is who controlled the term.
Here is who bore the cost.
Here is who received the gain.
Here is what the arrangement was called.
Here is what the evidence says instead or more completely.
Here is the strongest fact that cuts against our reading.
Here is what remains unknown.
```

This is how the Harm Mesh becomes public evidence rather than a demand for trust.
