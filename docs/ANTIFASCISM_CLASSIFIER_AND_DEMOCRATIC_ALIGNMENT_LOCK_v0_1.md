# Antifascism Classifier and Democratic-Alignment Lock v0.1

**Status:** ACTIVE / CROSS-SURFACE CLASSIFIER LOCK  
**Introduced:** 2026-08-27  
**Purpose:** prevent government, prosecutorial, media, repository, or security labels from silently converting antifascism into terrorism, extremism, criminality, or initiating aggression.

## Core distinction

Antifascism, at the principle level, is opposition to fascist domination and the destruction of plural political life. Opposition to fascism is compatible with and can function in defense of democratic conditions such as pluralism, equal political standing, opposition, association, accountable government, and peaceful removability of rulers.

This does **not** mean every person, organization, tactic, or act using an antifascist label is automatically democratic, lawful, defensive, or harmless.

It means the project must not invert the classifier:

```text
antifascist identity
!= terrorism
!= extremism
!= criminality
!= formal organizational membership
!= initiating aggression
```

## Government and institutional labels

If a president, department, prosecutor, police body, intelligence service, military, legislature, media outlet, or other authority calls Antifa or an antifascist actor `terrorist`, `extremist`, `violent`, `criminal`, or an equivalent term, the statement establishes first that the authority **used that classifier**.

It does not independently establish the classifier's truth.

```text
official designation
-> STATED / OBSERVED CLASSIFIER EVENT
!= independent terrorism finding
!= proof of organization
!= proof of membership
!= proof of conduct
!= proof of causal role
```

Any terrorism or violent-extremism classification must separately establish the relevant conduct, actor identity, intent/mental state where required, organizational relation where claimed, target, chronology, statutory or analytical predicate, and evidentiary/adjudicative posture.

## Repository-seating lock

A file about an antifascist person, protest, prosecution, government designation, or state surveillance may legitimately appear in `UNUM-Extremism-Watch` because the **extremism classifier itself**, an extremist opponent, political violence, state repression, or a comparative extremism question is under study.

Therefore:

```text
appears_in_Extremism_Watch
!= classified_as_extremist
```

Repository location is custody/routing metadata, not moral, ideological, legal, or causal classification.

## Causal-role lock

For fascism, authoritarian repression, occupation, racist political violence, political detention, or other prior coercive conditions, restore the prior field before classifying visible resistance.

```text
prior coercive condition
-> antifascist / democratic resistance
```

must not be silently rewritten as:

```text
antifascist resistance
-> initiating threat
-> state / fascist actor becomes defender
```

Use `schemas/AGGRESSION_DEFENSE_RESISTANCE_PASSPORT_v0_1.yaml` for every consequential force case.

A later independently evidenced harmful act by a resisting actor remains separately condemnable and prosecutable. It does not retroactively convert fascism into defense or transfer an earlier initiating coercive edge.

## Organization lock

`Antifa` is frequently used as an umbrella political label. Do not infer a single command structure, stable membership list, governance system, funding hierarchy, or operational network merely from shared antifascist identity, black-bloc tactics, symbols, rhetoric, protest attendance, association, or government terminology.

Any claimed organization must be established from organization-specific evidence.

## Historical and normative inversion lock

The project must never reason as though opposition to fascism supplies evidence in favor of fascism.

```text
antifascist resistance exists
!= fascist actor justified
antifascist actor commits later wrongdoing
!= fascist project becomes legitimate
state labels antifascism terrorism
!= fascism becomes democratic defense
```

This applies symmetrically to historical and contemporary analysis. Nazi Germany, fascist movements, and other systems are assessed through their documented ideology, coercion, aggression, exterminatory or exclusionary projects, institutional structure, and acts—not rehabilitated through misconduct by opponents.

## Evidence rule

Individual antifascist actors receive no blanket immunity. Individual state, fascist, or antifascist actors receive no automatic guilt from identity alone.

The rule is:

> **Antifascism is not a terrorism/extremism predicate. Conduct earns conduct classifications. Organization earns organization classifications. Official labels remain attributed until independently established. Restore prior coercion before assigning aggressor and defender roles.**
