# UNUM-Lattice-News — Controversy Membrane v0.1

Status: living registry / central transform

## Purpose
This is both a topic list and a transformation membrane. Topics must not disappear merely because their detailed investigations live elsewhere. Each topic can expand indefinitely into actors, victims, authorities, institutions, evidence, money, courts, media, movements, harms, protections, and downstream consequences.

The membrane transforms the media label `controversy` into a human-readable question:

> What is actually being protected, what is actually being harmed, who has power, who bears the cost, and what evidence supports each claim?

A topic being listed here does not imply moral or evidentiary symmetry.

## Transform
For every topic:

```text
TOPIC / EVENT / POLICY
-> public controversy framing
-> competing protection claims
-> actual protected objects
-> alleged harms
-> documented/measured harms
-> victims / affected populations
-> authority seats
-> institutions
-> money / donors / contractors
-> ideology / movement / religious / nationalist interfaces
-> evidence and record control
-> courts / appeal / oversight
-> media / classifier framing
-> who gains power
-> who loses agency
-> counterevidence / falsification
-> unresolved unknowns and why they are unknown
```

## Public-facing rendering
For each mature topic, the eventual public surface should answer in plain language:
1. What are people told the controversy is?
2. What protections does each actor claim?
3. What harms are actually documented?
4. Who has the power to impose those harms or protections?
5. Who bears the downside risk?
6. Who profits or gains authority?
7. Who controls the evidence and definitions?
8. What do courts or oversight bodies actually establish — and what do they not establish?
9. What evidence cuts against our current model?
10. What remains unknown, and why?

## Growth rule
Never delete a topic merely because it is inactive. Mark temporal state and retain lineage. Add subtopics when a topic becomes too compressed. Link every detailed investigation back to this membrane so the system remains navigable.
