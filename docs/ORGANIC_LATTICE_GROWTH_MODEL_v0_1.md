# Organic Lattice Growth Model v0.1

Status: active review
Legacy source branch: `organic-lattice-sorting-2026-08-11`

## Purpose

UNUM Lattice News is a growing evidence ecology, not a fixed database ontology. Structure should emerge from repeated use while preserving provenance, reversibility, reviewability, and the harm-audit methodology.

The repository may therefore grow at several scales at once:

```text
source evidence
  -> small records / seeds
  -> durable nodes and typed relations
  -> investigations
  -> renditions / reports
  -> corrections and re-entry
```

No layer replaces the others.

## Growth rule

Start with the smallest recoverable object that preserves the evidence.

Promote structure when repeated use justifies it.

Do not create a large node merely because a subject is important. Do not keep a recurring subject trapped in one investigation merely because that is where it first appeared.

## Three working scales

### 1. Seeds

Seeds are small, narrowly scoped records. They may represent:

- a source;
- a dated story or event;
- a typed relation;
- an assessment;
- a correction;
- a prospective scenario branch.

Seeds should be cheap to add, easy to review, and reusable across multiple nodes or investigations.

### 2. Durable nodes

Nodes are persistent subject surfaces: people, institutions, technologies, policies, movements, places, populations, ecologies, propositions, and other recurring subjects.

A node should grow when evidence repeatedly returns to the same subject. A large node may become a local index and split independently important lanes into subnodes while retaining parent relations.

### 3. Investigations and renditions

Investigations are question-shaped working surfaces. Reports and renditions are dated projections of the lattice for a reader or decision.

They may synthesize many seeds and nodes, but should not become the only canonical home of reusable evidence.

## Natural sorting

Sorting is iterative rather than totalizing.

When reviewing an investigation or report, ask:

1. Which claims are reusable outside this document?
2. Which recurring actors or subjects deserve durable nodes?
3. Which relationships deserve independent typed relation records?
4. Which events deserve dated story seeds?
5. Which assessments are stateful and should remain separate from identity?
6. Which material is still too uncertain to promote?
7. Which harmful mechanism recurs across otherwise different actors or domains?
8. Which gain, loss, gate, lever, or accountability-failure object is being duplicated in prose and should become reusable structure?

Material may remain where it is until reuse pressure justifies extraction.

## Routing membranes

Hashtags and categories are routing surfaces, not evidence and not containment boxes.

The same record may participate in multiple neighborhoods without duplication. Examples:

```text
#actor/person
#institution/government
#mechanism/capture
#mechanism/retaliation
#resource/oil
#right/sovereignty
#domain/campaign-finance
#status/alleged
#status/observed
```

Hashtag co-occurrence does not establish a relation.

## Assessment separation

Trust, threat, harm, beneficiary gain, and accountability remain distinct.

Assessment attaches to a scoped relation/state and evidence set rather than becoming an identity label.

```text
TRUST != THREAT != HARM != GUILT != IDENTITY != MORAL GOODNESS
BENEFICIARY_GAIN != MORAL GOODNESS
```

A score may focus attention. It may never decide alone.

In the current harm-audit lane, unrelated beneficial conduct is out of scope. A gain is recorded when it is causally or distributionally connected to the harmful mechanism under study: money, assets, access, authority, insulation, capacity, cost externalization, or another evidenced advantage.

## Promotion pressure

Consider extracting a reusable seed or node when one or more of these recur:

- the same actor appears in multiple investigations;
- the same relation is cited more than once;
- a dated event changes several nodes;
- a source supports several claims;
- an assessment needs independent revision history;
- a lane inside a large node becomes independently important;
- a report would otherwise duplicate evidence already established elsewhere;
- the same harmful mechanism appears across actors or institutions;
- the same harmed population or shared-harm object risks being counted repeatedly;
- the same gate, lever, beneficiary-gain path, loss path, or blocked accountability route recurs.

These are prompts, not automatic thresholds.

## Harm-audit promotion rule

Promotion does not mean accusation becomes fact.

```text
repeated mention -> candidate structure
candidate structure + evidence -> typed relation / node / claim-test object
confirmed scoped contribution -> harm hierarchy input
```

Do not promote:

- identity adjacency into guilt;
- network proximity into control;
- campaign money into purchased action without the missing causal edge;
- official framing into demonstrated outcome;
- repeated allegation into independent corroboration;
- institutional importance into automatic culpability.

Do promote reusable mechanisms when evidence repeatedly supports them, including:

- gatekeeping;
- procurement routing;
- donor/access networks;
- regulatory or judicial insulation;
- retaliation and correction blockage;
- cost externalization;
- personnel crossings;
- concentration of authority;
- shared-harm responsibility structures.

## Re-entry and correction

Extraction must preserve the path back to the originating investigation/report and underlying sources.

If later evidence changes a seed, relation, node, or assessment, dependent renditions should be discoverable for review.

Do not silently rewrite historical projections. Preserve what was known, what changed, and when.

A later finding that narrows or defeats a harm claim must return through the same lineage. Correction is not positive balancing; it is part of adversarial evidentiary integrity.

## Core locks

```text
Grow from evidence, not appetite.
Small records may become organs; organs may split when function demands it.
Categories route; they do not convict.
Investigations ask questions; nodes preserve reusable state.
Reports are projections, not the substrate itself.
Nothing crosses naked.
Returnability outranks apparent coherence.
The lattice may evolve without pretending its earlier forms never existed.
Unrelated beneficial conduct does not offset documented harm.
Claim-specific counterevidence must remain able to change the model.
```
