## Core correction

Do not treat left, labor, progressive, socialist, anti-fascist, immigrant-rights, environmental, or public-interest complaints as one ideology. Treat each complaint as an entry seat and ask what material mechanism it names.

Recurring complaint families recovered from current AFL-CIO, Working Families Party, DSA, EPI, and Public Citizen sources include:

- wages failing to keep up with costs and productivity;
- weak worker bargaining power, union suppression, misclassification, and precarious work;
- rent burden, landlord concentration, and lack of affordable/social housing;
- healthcare organized around private profit, monopoly, denial, high prices, and job dependency;
- monopoly, private equity, merger concentration, and corporate political power;
- corporate tax advantages, subsidies, contracts, guarantees, public finance, and privatization;
- immigrant scapegoating and immigration enforcement that can increase worker vulnerability;
- ecological and climate costs externalized onto communities and future generations;
- war/security spending and extractive foreign policy;
- voting, speech, association, protest, union, watchdog, and other democratic-redress concerns.

The recurrence of a complaint does not prove its causal story. It justifies promoting the mechanism into the research queue.

## Ideological threat-labeling crossing

Current White House material provides direct examples of:

- Antifa designated by executive order as a domestic terrorist organization;
- self-described anti-fascism placed inside a broader domestic-terrorism narrative;
- a 2026 global campaign framed around Radical Left terrorism;
- equity/DEI spending described in Marxist or harmful ideological terms;
- socialism and communism described as evil ideologies that must not take root domestically.

The mesh must distinguish violent crime from ideology, association, lawful protest, civil-liberties claims, funding relationships, and nonviolent collective redress.

Earlier CRS analysis states that domestic terrorism is defined in federal law but is not itself a standalone federal charge and historically lacked the formal domestic-organization designation mechanism used for foreign terrorist organizations. Current executive designation language must therefore be tracked separately from specific statutory criminal authority.

## Corporate welfare / corporate communism translation

`corporate communism` is preserved as a user/public complaint label, not adopted as a technical economic classification.

The measurable mechanism is:

```text
public money / credit / tax preference / insurance / infrastructure / monopoly protection
-> privately controlled enterprise
-> public value delivered or not delivered
-> downside risk allocation
-> retained private ownership and upside
-> executive / creditor / shareholder / asset gain
-> political and market power feedback
```

Call candidate **asymmetric socialization** when the public absorbs substantial cost or downside while private owners retain disproportionate upside without commensurate public return.

Baseline evidence already recovered:

- GAO: about $793B in federal contract obligations in FY2025;
- Treasury: tax expenditures are revenue losses functioning as alternative policy instruments;
- GAO: tax expenditures can have the same net budget effect as spending and often avoid annual appropriations review;
- GAO: $30.9B in semiconductor incentive awards across 40 projects to 19 companies by July 2025;
- DFC/EXIM and other public-finance rails already entered in the external-resource/statecraft mesh.

Contracts are not automatically welfare. Tax expenditures are not automatically corporate. Industrial policy can create genuine public value. The mesh must trace competition, conditions, risk, public return, labor standards, price pass-through, ownership return, and political feedback before classifying a transfer.

## Hypothesis to test, not presume

```text
material grievance
-> collective redress demand
-> ideological/security threat label
-> public fear / legitimacy loss
-> enforcement or exclusion
-> weakened redress capacity
-> underlying concentrated arrangement persists
```

This is a supported mechanism hypothesis, not a universal motive attribution. Search for both confirming cases and countercases where threat labels are narrowly tied to violent or unlawful conduct and do not suppress lawful redress.
