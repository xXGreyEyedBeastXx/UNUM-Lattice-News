## Audit method

For each surface ask:

1. Is it an owning detailed surface or an intentionally compressed routing surface?
2. Are date/time and sequence retained where they change the inference?
3. Are actor, institution, command seat and personnel kinship retained without merger?
4. Are source posture and claim posture recoverable?
5. Are official counterpositions preserved without automatic evidentiary priority?
6. Are alternative causal models and missing discriminators retained?
7. Does a later synthesis silently upgrade allegation -> finding -> intent -> command?
8. Does a later summary erase a distinction preserved earlier?
9. Can a future analyst reach the source custody without reproducing the investigation from scratch?
10. Does the surface point to a fuller owner when it intentionally compresses detail?
