## Material extraction spine already recovered

### Labor
- wage theft is a direct form of extraction;
- employer concentration and anticompetitive labor practices can suppress wages without wage-theft illegality;
- legal wage != competitively formed or adequate wage;
- low compensation can coexist with worker-household use of Medicaid, SNAP, housing support, refundable credits, or other public support;
- public support legally benefits the household while potentially reducing the private compensation/benefit burden necessary to reproduce labor; employer incidence must be measured rather than assumed;
- compensation, public supplementation, corporate surplus, executive pay, dividends, buybacks, retained earnings, and ownership must be recomposed in one circuit.

They do establish that worker compensation, public supplementation, firm surplus, executive compensation, shareholder distributions, and concentrated ownership coexist in the same corporate circuit and must not be falsely disconnected.

## Price extraction / supposedly competitive pricing

The mesh must test the claim that a price is competitive rather than accept the label.

Required trace:

```text
cost change
-> price change
-> market concentration
-> realistic substitution / exit
-> coordination / common algorithms / information sharing
-> gross and operating margin
-> surplus disposition
-> ownership return
```

Recovered research surfaces include:
- FTC grocery-supply-chain work finding cases where firms used cost shocks and disrupted markets to raise prices beyond cost changes while profits remained elevated;
- DOJ/RealPage enforcement showing that rent described as market/competitive may involve common nonpublic information and algorithmic coordination.

High price alone != price gouging. `competitive price` alone != evidence of competitive formation.

## Immigration blame / extraction inversion

Core working relation:

```text
UPWARD MATERIAL FLOW <-> DOWNWARD BLAME FLOW
```

Do not reduce this to "immigration has no economic effects." CBO material already recovered shows real localized/short-run pressures can occur, including wage and housing-demand effects. The mesh question is larger:

```text
pressure
-> all causal contributors
-> who controls terms
-> who captures wage savings / rent / margin / public contract / ownership gain
-> who publicly assigns blame
```

Recovered inversion:

- GEO Group and CoreCivic became major ICE-revenue-dependent corporate nodes in current reporting/filings;
- selected detention labor litigation shows immigrants can be accused of consuming taxpayer resources while confined immigrants' labor and taxpayer-funded detention both feed a corporate operating circuit.

Reusable operator promoted from this thread: `downward-scapegoat-upward-extraction-inversion`.

## Redress, legitimacy, and violence prevention

The mesh exists in part to make harmful systems legible while peaceful democratic correction remains possible.

Trace:

```text
harm
-> complaint / vote / union / lawsuit / appeal / regulator / press / protest / petition / boycott / strike
-> correction returns or fails
-> legitimacy changes
-> nonviolent collective alternatives expand or contract
-> violence risk rises if meaningful return paths collapse
```

Institutional failure can increase violence risk without removing individual agency or making violence inevitable/legitimate.

Elections are a critical correction path. Current election-administration research must distinguish:
- observed rhetoric and policy actions;
- attempts to expand executive/federal control;
- documented pressure/bypass of election institutions;
- coercive-capacity expansions;
- unestablished hypotheses about a coordinated plan to determine future election outcomes.

The mesh should alert before a worst-case hypothesis is proven, but it must preserve state: `observed`, `candidate crossing`, `not established`, `contradicted`.
