# Self-Investigation, Independence, and Accountability v0.1

Status: ACTIVE METHODOLOGY
Date: 2026-08-17

## Root rule

An institution's investigation of itself is evidence. It is not automatically independent adjudication.

```text
SELF_INVESTIGATION != INDEPENDENT_REVIEW
SELF_EXONERATION != DEBUNKING
FORMAL_REVIEW_MECHANISM != EFFECTIVE_ACCOUNTABILITY
INTERNAL_INDEPENDENCE_CLAIM != EXTERNAL_INDEPENDENCE_FINDING
```

The evidentiary weight of an internal finding depends on the independence, access, transparency, adversarial testing, correction record, enforcement capacity, and demonstrated outcomes of the mechanism that produced it.

## Accountability is observable

Accountability is not proven because an institution says it has procedures. Test whether the system can actually:

- investigate senior leadership as well as low-level personnel;
- compel records and testimony;
- preserve evidence independently of the operational chain;
- protect witnesses, complainants, investigators and dissenters;
- publish enough reasoning to permit external scrutiny;
- distinguish operational lessons from criminal/legal responsibility;
- impose consequences proportionate to supported findings;
- reopen cases when contrary evidence appears;
- accept adverse findings from courts, inspectors, commissions or other outside bodies;
- resist political or command retaliation;
- retain investigators who produce inconvenient findings.

## Credibility / accountability decay

A self-review system loses evidentiary weight when a repeated pattern appears:

```text
serious allegation or external evidence
    -> institution announces internal review
    -> review is delayed, opaque, narrowed or closed
    -> little or no consequence follows
    -> materially contradictory external evidence emerges
    -> institution repeats the same self-review assurance
```

This does not make every internal finding false. It changes the prior weight assigned to future self-exoneration.

Preferred type:

`SELF_EXONERATION_CREDIBILITY_DECAY`

Evidence requirements:

- repeated internal findings or closures;
- recoverable outcome data where possible;
- external evidence or findings materially inconsistent with internal conclusions;
- evidence about independence, staffing, access or political/command interference;
- correction behavior when internal claims are contradicted.

## Independence is relational, not ceremonial

An office can be formally separated from an operational chain while remaining institutionally dependent through appointment, budget, staffing, promotion, access, shared personnel, political control, or review structure.

```text
FORMAL_SEPARATION != PRACTICAL_INDEPENDENCE
DIFFERENT_OFFICE != DIFFERENT_POWER_SYSTEM
```

Test:

1. who appoints/removes leadership;
2. who controls budget and staffing;
3. who controls evidence access;
4. whether personnel circulate between investigator and investigated bodies;
5. whether findings can bind or sanction the investigated body;
6. whether outside review is genuinely available;
7. whether adverse investigators are protected or removed;
8. whether senior leadership is realistically reachable.

## Israel / IDF specimen

The IDF states that its Military Advocate General is professionally independent and that the General Staff Fact-Finding Assessment Mechanism is detached from the operational chain. Those facts belong in the record.

They do not settle effectiveness or independence. External investigations and monitoring have repeatedly raised concerns about impunity, lack of accountability, or low rates of consequential outcomes. The evidentiary task is therefore not `IDF says independent -> independent`, nor `internal -> automatically worthless`, but comparison of mandate, personnel, process, outcomes, external contradiction and correction response.

A particularly important discriminator is whether an internal inquiry merely identifies an `operational failure` while external evidence supports a stronger legal or intentional classification.

## Trump-administration specimen

Independent oversight in the United States provides a second, structurally useful example.

When inspectors general, professional-responsibility staff, career prosecutors or other watchdogs are removed, depleted, made dependent on agency leadership, or replaced by officials with strong prior loyalty/administration ties, later internal assurances must be weighted in light of that changed accountability architecture.

This does not make every Trump-administration investigation false. It means the independence variable cannot be assumed constant after the oversight system itself changes.

Current investigation should separately track:

- removal of independent watchdogs;
- vacancies and staffing losses;
- political/loyalty relationships of replacements;
- White House involvement in individual enforcement matters;
- departures or retaliation affecting career investigators/prosecutors;
- judicial findings contradicting executive or DOJ accounts;
- whether internal bodies investigate senior officials despite political cost.

## Trust consequence

Trust is an empirical output of correction behavior, not a moral credit balance.

```text
ERROR + CORRECTION + CONSEQUENCE -> trust can recover
ERROR + DENIAL + SELF_CLEARANCE + REPETITION -> trust should decrease
EXTERNAL_CONTRADICTION + NO_REOPENING -> further decrease
REMOVAL_OF_WATCHDOGS -> independence risk increases
```

Do not convert low institutional trust into automatic falsity of every statement. Instead require stronger independent corroboration for consequential self-serving claims.

## Re-entry lock

When a powerful actor says `we investigated ourselves and found no wrongdoing`, record at least:

- who investigated;
- institutional relationship to the investigated actor;
- appointment/removal relationship;
- evidence available and unavailable;
- whether victims/independent witnesses participated;
- published reasoning;
- outcome/consequence;
- external contrary findings;
- whether the case was reopened after contradiction;
- current credibility posture of the reviewing mechanism.

**Accountability is demonstrated by the system's ability to produce and survive adverse findings against itself.**
