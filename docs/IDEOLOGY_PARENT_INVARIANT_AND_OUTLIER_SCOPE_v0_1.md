# Ideology Parent Invariant and Outlier Scope v0.1

Status: ACTIVE METHODOLOGY CORRECTION  
Date: 2026-08-17

## Purpose

Prevent a recurring attribution error in which a reformer, dissident, unusually humane participant, or self-described subtype is used to redefine or morally offset the parent ideology or movement.

The lattice must preserve both of these facts when they are evidenced:

1. a movement can contain real internal disagreements; and
2. an outlier does not become representative merely because they retain the movement's label.

## Core rule

**A dissident does not falsify the parent structure merely by remaining inside the parent label.**

Formally:

`SELF_IDENTIFIES_AS(X) != REPRESENTATIVE_INSTANTIATION_OF(X)`

`OUTLIER_WITHIN(X) != COUNTEREVIDENCE_TO_PARENT_INVARIANT(X)`

`SUBTYPE_DIFFERENCE != ABSENCE_OF_PARENT_IDEOLOGY`

A subtype or branch is analytically useful when it describes a genuine difference in program, tactics, institutions, or desired outcomes. It must not be used as an automatic exculpatory membrane around the shared parent commitments that remain intact.

## Required outlier typing

When citing a person or faction whose position substantially departs from the movement's dominant or institutional program, explicitly record:

- relationship to the movement;
- self-identification, if relevant;
- which parent commitments they retain;
- which parent commitments they reject or materially transform;
- whether their position was mainstream, minority, dissident, opposition, or unresolved in the relevant period;
- whether the example is being used to demonstrate internal contestation or to make a claim about the movement as a whole.

Do not silently move from `EXISTS_WITHIN_MOVEMENT` to `REPRESENTS_MOVEMENT`.

## Laundering failure: representative-exception substitution

A common analytical failure is:

`movement has harmful or exclusionary invariant`

→ `one humane/reformist actor retains movement label`

→ `actor rejects or softens invariant`

→ **incorrect conclusion:** `therefore the parent movement is non-exclusionary / harmless / morally plural in the relevant respect`.

This is invalid unless there is evidence that the dissident position actually became representative of the movement, its governing institutions, or the relevant population under study.

The correct conclusion may instead be:

`INTERNAL_DISSENT_EXISTS`

while the parent movement's dominant/institutional structure remains independently evidenced.

## Zionism specimen

For the present lattice, Zionism is first seated as a nationalist movement. The World Zionist Organization's official Jerusalem Program defines Zionism as the national liberation movement of the Jewish people and centers a Jewish, Zionist State of Israel, Jewish national continuity, aliyah, national Zionist interests, and settlement. Israel's 2018 Nation-State Basic Law separately provides a current state-law expression in which national self-determination in Israel is unique to the Jewish people and Jewish settlement is a national value.

This classification must remain distinct from Judaism, Jewish identity, Jewish people, Israeli citizenship, the State of Israel, a particular Israeli government, and the conduct or beliefs of any individual Jew.

### Martin Buber scope correction

Martin Buber may be cited as evidence that **internal Zionist dissent existed**. He must not be cited as representative counterevidence against the dominant Zionist state-building program.

The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy records that Buber:

- participated deeply in Zionist institutions and retained a Zionist self-identification;
- increasingly opposed the dominant state-building trajectory;
- rejected pursuit of Jewish sovereign statehood and Jewish majority rule over the Arab population;
- advocated demographic parity and a binational state;
- understood that these views were unlikely to sway the Zionist majority and later occupied a position of opposition to the Zionist establishment.

Therefore the correct lattice typing is approximately:

`BUBER SELF_IDENTIFIES_WITH ZIONISM` — historically supported

`BUBER PARTICIPATES_IN ZIONIST_MOVEMENT` — historically supported

`BUBER REPRESENTS_DOMINANT_POLITICAL_ZIONISM` — contradicted by his documented opposition

`BUBER INTERNAL_DISSIDENT_WITHIN ZIONIST_MOVEMENT` — supported

`BUBER_BINATIONALISM PROVES_PARENT_ZIONISM_NONEXCLUSIONARY` — invalid inference

His example demonstrates contestation and attempted reform, not representativeness.

## Accountability rule

A person's incomplete understanding of an ideology can affect what can responsibly be inferred about their knowledge or intent. It does not erase the causal effect of material support, advocacy, voting, funding, normalization, institutional participation, or other demonstrated contribution.

Keep separate:

- `IDEOLOGICAL_SUPPORT`
- `KNOWLEDGE`
- `INTENT`
- `CAUSAL_CONTRIBUTION`
- `CORRECTION_RESPONSE`

Do not infer full intent from mere affiliation. Do not infer causal innocence from ignorance either.

## Locks

- Do not use one good actor to morally average a harmful movement.
- Do not use one dissident to redefine a dominant institutional program.
- Preserve genuine internal disagreement without promoting it to representativeness.
- A self-applied label does not override the actor's documented program.
- Branches may describe modalities; they do not automatically erase the parent invariant.
- Claims about exclusion, supremacy, discrimination, displacement, violence, or other harms still require their own evidence chains.
- Keep people, religion, ethnicity, ideology, state, government, organization, and conduct separately typed.
