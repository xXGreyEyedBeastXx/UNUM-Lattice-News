## Aggression / defense / resistance lock

Do not begin a conflict record at the moment resistance becomes visible.

Reconstruct:

```text
prior condition
-> initiating coercion / aggression
-> response
-> counter-response
-> later escalation / cessation / repair
```

Do not infer:

```text
state actor -> defender
recognized military -> lawful defense
resister -> initiating aggressor
protester -> nonviolent by definition
terrorism label -> terrorism established
security label -> protection established
```

Classify acts separately and preserve ongoing threat, necessity, proportionality, civilian targeting, territorial/coercive context, available exit/redress, retaliatory/punitive components, and power asymmetry.

Use:

```text
schemas/AGGRESSION_DEFENSE_RESISTANCE_PASSPORT_v0_1.yaml
```
