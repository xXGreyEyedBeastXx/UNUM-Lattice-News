## 1. Organic Lattice Growth Model v0.1

Status: active review

### Purpose

UNUM Lattice News is a growing evidence ecology, not a fixed database ontology. Structure should emerge from repeated use while preserving provenance, reversibility, and reviewability.

The repository may therefore grow at several scales at once:

```text
source evidence
  -> small records / seeds
  -> durable nodes and typed relations
  -> investigations
  -> renditions / reports
  -> corrections and re-entry
```

No layer replaces the others.

### Growth rule

Start with the smallest recoverable object that preserves the evidence.

Promote structure when repeated use justifies it.

Do not create a large node merely because a subject is important. Do not keep a recurring subject trapped in one investigation merely because that is where it first appeared.

### Three working scales

#### Seeds

Seeds are small, narrowly scoped records. They may represent a source, dated story/event, typed relation, assessment, correction, or prospective scenario branch. Seeds should be cheap to add, easy to review, and reusable.

#### Durable nodes

Nodes are persistent subject surfaces. A node should grow when evidence repeatedly returns to the same subject. Large nodes may split independently important lanes while retaining parent relations.

#### Investigations and renditions

Investigations are question-shaped working surfaces. Reports/renditions are dated projections of the lattice. They may synthesize seeds and nodes, but should not become the only canonical home of reusable evidence.

### Natural sorting

Sorting is iterative rather than totalizing. Ask which claims are reusable, which actors deserve durable nodes, which relations deserve independent records, which events deserve seeds, which assessments need separate revision history, and which material remains too uncertain to promote.

### Routing membranes

Hashtags and categories are routing surfaces, not evidence and not containment boxes. Hashtag co-occurrence does not establish a relation.

### Assessment separation

```text
TRUST != THREAT != HARM != GUILT != IDENTITY
```

Assessment attaches to a scoped relation/state and evidence set rather than becoming an identity label. A score may focus attention; it may never decide alone.

### Promotion pressure

Consider extraction when actors recur across investigations, relations recur, dated events change several nodes, sources support several claims, assessments need independent revision, lanes inside a node become independently important, or reports would otherwise duplicate established evidence.

### Re-entry and correction

Extraction must preserve the path back to the originating investigation/report and sources. Do not silently rewrite historical projections; preserve what was known, what changed, and when.

### Core locks

```text
Grow from evidence, not appetite.
Small records may become organs; organs may split when function demands it.
Categories route; they do not convict.
Investigations ask questions; nodes preserve reusable state.
Reports are projections, not the substrate itself.
Nothing crosses naked.
Returnability outranks apparent coherence.
The lattice may evolve without pretending its earlier forms never existed.
```

## 2. Identity, Ideology, Organization, Conduct, and Sponsorship Classification v0.1

Status: active review

### Core rule

```text
IDENTITY != IDEOLOGY != ORGANIZATION != ROLE != TACTIC != SPONSORSHIP != EVIDENTIARY STATUS
```

This prevents category errors such as Muslim→Islamist→jihadist→insurgent→terrorist; Christian→Christian nationalist→extremist→violent actor; Jewish→Zionist→Israeli state actor; state military→lawful conduct; insurgent→terrorist; protester→extremist. None of these arrows may be assumed.

Identity alone does not establish ideology, political program, conduct, threat, or responsibility. Ideology does not itself establish violence or crime. Organizational form and legal designation remain distinct from conduct. Role is event-local and does not totalize identity.

Conduct must be classified from observed or alleged acts separately from identity and organization. State sponsorship must not be inferred from ideological alignment or geographic proximity. Statehood does not sanitize conduct.

### Terrorism / insurgency distinction

```text
INSURGENCY != TERRORISM
ARMED_RESISTANCE != TERRORISM
STATE_FORCE != LAWFUL_CONDUCT
TERRORIST_DESIGNATION != PROOF_OF_EVERY_ALLEGED_ACT
```

Insurgency is an organizational/conflict role; terrorism is a tactic/strategy classification. Preserve governing definitions, jurisdiction, evidence posture, and dispute.

### Religious-nationalist application

For any religion or political identity, keep identity, theological tradition, political advocacy, nationalism, organization, rhetoric, coercive policy support, violence support, and participation in violence separately evidenced.

### Evidentiary status

```text
observed
stated
self_identified
supported_inference
alleged
disputed
adjudicated
unknown
missing_or_withheld
```

Threat assessment follows classification and should derive from capability, conduct, trajectory, constraints, reach, vulnerability, irreversibility, opacity, repetition, escalation potential, repair capacity, and immediacy—not identity.

### Core locks

```text
Identity is not tactic.
Ideology is not conduct.
Insurgency is not terrorism.
Statehood is not innocence.
Designation is not proof.
Religious identity is not collective culpability.
Threat is assessed from capability, conduct, trajectory, and constraint interaction.
No body is exempt from the evidence standard.
```

## 3. Dissent, Reform, and Internal Correction Diagnostic v0.1

Status: active review

### Purpose

Evaluate what a group, institution, movement, ideology, religion, party, corporation, state, or community actually protects by examining how it treats internal dissent, reform, whistleblowing, defections, moral criticism, and corrective pressure.

### Core proposition

```text
An ideology tells us what it claims to value.
Its treatment of internal reformers tells us what it protects under pressure.
```

Internal dissent is not automatically correct. Evaluate both the content of dissent and the system response.

### Correction permeability

**Correction permeability** is how easily accurate criticism can cross a group's defensive boundary and produce durable change.

High correction permeability may include protected criticism channels, independent review, transparent corrections, leadership accountability, minority-position preservation, reversible decisions, acknowledgement of error, and restoration after unjust punishment.

Low correction permeability may include loyalty tests, leader infallibility, purity enforcement, retaliation against whistleblowers, counterevidence suppression, forced unanimity, reputational destruction, redefining criticism as betrayal, punishment without independent appeal, and historical rewriting after failure.

Repeated punishment of dissent can indicate capture, but the inference must remain typed and should require recurrence, asymmetry, leadership insulation, suppression of evidence, and failure to correct known harms.

### Reform stress test

Ask what happens when adherents try to remove domination, cruelty, corruption, coercion, hierarchy, or factual error. Outcomes may include integration, plural coexistence, marginal tolerance, boundary expulsion, suppression, or semantic departure.

### Core locks

```text
Dissent is not automatically truth.
Conformity is not automatically loyalty.
Criticism is not automatically betrayal.
A group's treatment of critics is evidence about its power structure.
Valid reform must be allowed to change the model.
No organization may be sole judge of its own harms.
Internal critics retain dignity even when they are wrong.
Whistleblower protection does not erase accountability for false claims.
Re-entry matters.
Apply this diagnostic to us too.
```

## 4. Trust & Threat Assessment Field v0.1

**Status:** working infrastructure; non-canonical scoring model

### Core separation

```text
TRUST != THREAT != HARM != GUILT != IDENTITY
```

Trust asks whether a bounded source/claim deserves epistemic weight. Threat asks capability, direction, trajectory, and agency constraint. Harm asks consequences. Accountability asks how choice, knowledge, power, alternatives, repetition, and causal contribution intersect.

### Instrument law

```text
observation -> dimensions -> evidence -> score/range -> review -> re-entry
```

A score is an instrument output, not a moral identity and not permission to escalate. Scores must expose dimensions, weights, missing inputs, uncertainty, and correction history.

Trust is claim/domain/time-local rather than a universal reputation number. Recommended dimensions include authenticity, provenance, evidence recoverability, independence/interest, domain competence, historical reliability, correction/re-entry, transparency, and adversarial survivability. Repeated citation descendants do not become independent corroboration.

Threat is capability/trajectory/constraint-local rather than an enemy designation. Recommended dimensions include capability, expressed direction, trajectory, constraint erosion, reach, target vulnerability, irreversibility, opacity, precedent/repetition, escalation potential, repair capacity, and immediacy.

Do not collapse probability and severity into one number. Preserve current threat, escalation risk, and maximum plausible harm separately.

### Power / Agency / Harm coupling

```text
POWER MAP      -> capability / reach / constraint control
AGENCY MAP     -> vulnerability / alternatives / refusal space
HARM MAP       -> severity / irreversibility / affected populations
TRAJECTORY     -> direction / repetition / escalation
```

This is a projection layer, not a replacement for the underlying maps.

### Scoring discipline

Numeric scoring is optional. Missing data must not silently become zero; confidence remains separate from score; source trust does not mechanically determine claim truth; threat does not mechanically determine enforcement; no score may automatically trigger publication, targeting, punishment, surveillance, exposure, or escalation.

### Tiny locks

> Listen before scoring.

> Score the relation and state, not the soul.

> Trust is claim-local. Threat is trajectory-local. Harm is consequence-local. Dignity is not score-dependent.

> A score may focus attention. It may never decide alone.

## 5. News Automation Roadmap v0.1

**Status:** historical implementation TODO / source quarry

### Boundary

Automation may discover, organize, and draft. It may not establish consequential claims, promote nodes, or publish without required review.

### Durable design recovered

- resolve canonical node vocabulary before automated node work;
- use `mention -> candidate -> landed node` as anti-sprawl landing gate;
- empty landing results are valid;
- add a review-only story-draft packet between intake and nodes;
- preserve URLs, dates, source kind, posture, quotation boundaries, counterevidence, and unknowns;
- build deterministic structured reports before AI narrative;
- keep every generated paragraph recoverable to structured fields and source pointers;
- prefer static/review artifacts before database-backed/public systems;
- do not grant workflow tokens repository-write authority without a separately reviewed need;
- source quantity is not source diversity, and availability must not silently become editorial authority.

### Historical proposed sequence

```text
collect
-> validate feeds
-> produce intake artifact
-> verify and cluster sources
-> draft story packet
-> match mentions to existing nodes
-> propose candidates and relations
-> apply landing gate
-> validate structured report
-> render Markdown and HTML
-> upload review artifact
```

This sequence is preserved as historical design input only; current `main` architecture governs implementation.

## 7. Project 2025 / Heritage / Trump Policy Routing v0.1

**Status:** RECOVERED_ACTIVE_INVESTIGATION / REVERIFY BEFORE CURRENT PUBLIC USE

### Purpose

Trace relationships among the Heritage Foundation, Project 2025, former Trump personnel, Trump administrations, and implemented policy while distinguishing documented personnel/policy routing from unsupported centralized-control claims.

### Core locks

```text
Heritage Foundation != Trump administration
Project 2025 != every Trump policy
policy overlap != proof of direct instruction
personnel overlap != proof of total capture
documented routing != conspiracy inference
```

### Durable causal model

```text
Heritage / coalition organization
  -> Project 2025 role
  -> prior Trump administration role
  -> later administration appointment
  -> agency authority
  -> policy implementation
```

Do not infer causation solely from overlap; identify exact recommendations, appointment authority, implemented action, and dates.

### Competing causal models

1. Heritage directly shaped implementation through personnel and policy preparation.
2. Heritage mostly codified priorities Trump and allied conservatives already shared.
3. Heritage and Trump emerged from a broader coalition, producing correlated policy without centralized command.
4. Personnel circulation was the main transmission mechanism rather than formal policy adoption.
5. Some apparent policy matches have independent origins.

### Accountability questions

- Who wrote the recommendation?
- Did they later hold government power?
- Was the policy adopted substantially or only rhetorically?
- What changed in law, regulation, funding, staffing, or enforcement?
- Who gained discretion or power?
- Who lost benefits, due process, autonomy, or institutional protection?
- What counterevidence shows nonimplementation, modification, judicial blocking, or internal resistance?

### Recovered working hypothesis

Project 2025 is best tested as implementation infrastructure for a broader conservative governing coalition—policy development, personnel vetting, training, and early-action planning—and the Heritage–Trump relation as a routing network rather than a single-command hierarchy.

**Current-use lock:** all date-sensitive factual examples from the historical workbench must be reverified before publication or promotion. The methodological routing model is the primary preserved value.
