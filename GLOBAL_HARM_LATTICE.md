# Global Harm Lattice

**Status:** ACTIVE PUBLIC ROUTING SURFACE  
**As of:** 2026-08-28

This page is the streamlined public front door for the current global harm-and-power mesh.

It does **not** name every person, institution, company, armed group, donor, or intermediary. The sorting, intake, and specialist repositories exist to hold that depth. This surface keeps only the actors, theaters, systems, and crossings with enough centrality to help a reader understand how the larger field fits together.

## Core rule

```text
separate actors for attribution
+
reconnect systems for causality
```

Government, concentrated capital, billionaires, corporations, military power, ideology, technology, resources, geopolitics, and affected populations are not separate realities. They are distinct seats inside one causal field.

A repository boundary is not a causal boundary.

## Harm-causer protection versus harmed-person support

A central discriminator is whether the system offers more practical protection to the actor with power than to the population bearing the consequences.

```text
harm / imposed risk
-> affected population bears immediate cost
-> powerful actor accesses lawyers / money / politics / private resilience / reputation repair
-> accountability delayed or narrowed
-> harmed population carries proof / survival / waiting / recovery burden
-> upstream gain or capacity survives
-> repeat-harm capacity remains
```

Due process, mercy, and rehabilitation are not the problem.

The problem is **asymmetry**:

```text
context and second chances upward
suspicion and austerity downward
```

Punishment is not the same thing as repair. A system may punish someone symbolically while leaving the harmed population materially abandoned.

## Responsibility inversion and manufactured precarity

Another recurring mechanism under review is:

```text
power holder contributes to precarious condition
-> population becomes trapped in that condition
-> population is described as dependent / irresponsible / criminal / fraudulent / unproductive
-> punitive or exclusionary policy is justified
-> condition deepens
-> upstream gain, control, or insulation remains
```

Do not infer this mechanism from rhetoric alone. Actor-specific promotion requires separate evidence of the material contribution and the stigmatizing or punitive edge.

## Private exit / public risk

A system can become more coercive when those with the greatest rule-setting power are least dependent on the shared systems remaining healthy.

Trace:

```text
shared risk
-> public bears exposure
-> concentrated wealth buys private resilience and geographic/legal exit
-> lower-power populations remain dependent on shared gates
-> bargaining asymmetry increases
-> universal-system maintenance becomes less necessary to the insulated class
```

Private preparation does not prove desire for collapse. It becomes materially relevant when the same actor also helps set or preserve the terms producing the shared risk.

## Medieval / neo-feudal comparison gate

Technological advancement is not social progress by definition.

The medieval or neo-feudal analogy becomes stronger only where several of these are actually evidenced together:

- concentrated control of land or critical assets;
- survival access conditioned on relation to a power holder;
- weak meaningful exit;
- private or quasi-private security capacity;
- unequal legal or political access;
- self-reproducing concentration of power;
- tollbooth control over necessities;
- public-cost/private-gain feedback;
- patronage or dependency substituting for universal rights;
- accountability asymmetry;
- advanced technology without corresponding expansion of equal standing, remedy, consent, or practical freedom.

```text
modern inequality != feudalism by definition
mechanism match, not vibe
```
