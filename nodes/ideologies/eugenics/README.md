# Eugenics — Living Dossier

This directory is a relational investigation surface for historical and contemporary eugenic mechanisms, rhetoric, policy pathways, and ethical risk analysis.

It must not be used as a guilt-by-association label.

## Core distinction

```text
historical eugenics != every modern health policy
shared decision structure != identical historical program
risk pathway != proven intent
```

## Investigation lanes

- historical lineage and definitions
- disability and heredity
- reproductive policy and screening
- population-pathology rhetoric
- cost/dependency framing
- prevention versus treatment/support
- institutional and scientific authority
- modern bioethics and disability-rights critiques
- bridges to specific actors, institutions, and policies

## Evidence lock

An actor may be connected to this dossier through a supported inference about rhetoric, policy structure, or risk without being classified as a eugenicist absent evidence supporting that stronger claim.
