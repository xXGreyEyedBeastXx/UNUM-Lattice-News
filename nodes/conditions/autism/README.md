# Autism — Living Dossier

This directory is an additive dossier surface for the canonical autism node at `../../autism.yaml`.

It exists so autism can be read simultaneously as a medical/neurodevelopmental subject, a disability-and-support question, a public-policy target, a rhetoric object, and a node in broader harm/cultural-capture investigations without flattening those domains together.

## Required distinctions

- autism phenotype / diagnostic construct
- intellectual disability
- language impairment
- medical and psychiatric co-occurring conditions
- adaptive-function and support outcomes
- genetic and developmental evidence
- environmental-exposure hypotheses
- public rhetoric and policy framing
- neurodiversity and disability-rights perspectives

## Cross-boundary rule

Medical classification and clinical evidence should be anchored to UNUM-Medical. Lattice News may analyze rhetoric, policy, institutions, consequences, and public claims, but it should point back to the medical evidence surface when the truth of a medical proposition matters.
