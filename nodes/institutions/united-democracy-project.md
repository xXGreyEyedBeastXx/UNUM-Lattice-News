# United Democracy Project (UDP)

**Node type:** institution / super PAC / independent expenditure mechanism  
**Status:** active  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-12

## Identity

United Democracy Project (`FEC C00799031`) is an independent-expenditure-only federal super PAC associated with AIPAC's electoral strategy. It is distinct from AIPAC itself and from AIPAC PAC.

```text
AIPAC advocacy/lobbying
        |
        +--> AIPAC PAC: direct candidate contributions within federal limits
        |
        `--> UDP: independent expenditures supporting/opposing candidates
```

## Current financial capacity

FEC data for 2025 through 2026-05-31 reported approximately:

- $98.63 million total receipts;
- $95.80 million total contributions;
- $34.11 million total disbursements;
- $12.65 million independent expenditures during the coded reporting period;
- $93.32 million cash on hand.

These numbers establish unusually large electoral capacity. They do not establish the effect of any one expenditure without a race-specific record.

## Confirmed target pattern

AIPAC's own public materials say UDP played a significant or major role in defeating:

- Jamaal Bowman;
- Cori Bush;
- David Kim;

and describes those targets in explicitly anti-Israel terms.

Current reporting also documents major 2026 intervention in Democratic primaries involving candidates critical of AIPAC or U.S. military support to Israel, including the Michigan Senate primary.

## Harm-audit posture

UDP's independently exercisable political spending is a `power_concentration` and `democratic_epistemic_harm` investigation surface when it is used to overwhelm local campaign spending, punish dissent on war/occupation policy, or materially restructure candidate opportunity.

Do not score an election loss as solely caused by UDP unless evidence supports that counterfactual. Preserve:

```text
amount spent
message content
timing
local/candidate spending baseline
polling/turnout
candidate policy targeted
other major causes of outcome
```
