## Scope

This is an analytical system node for the second Trump administration and its federal executive machinery. It is not a claim that every agency, official, contractor, donor, congressional ally, state government, or foreign partner acts with one intention.

Use it when the relevant harm arises from administration-wide policy, coordinated executive action, cabinet/agency implementation, or an institutional change that cannot be responsibly attributed to Donald Trump alone.
