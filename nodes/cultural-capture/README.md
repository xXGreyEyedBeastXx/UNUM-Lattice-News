# Cultural Capture — Investigation Family

This node family tracks hypotheses and documented mechanisms by which institutions, professions, scientific agendas, media systems, religious movements, political networks, markets, or other cultural machinery may become disproportionately shaped by concentrated interests, ideology, incentives, authority, or coordinated pressure.

`cultural-capture` is not a default conclusion.

## Allowed posture

- `OBSERVED_INFLUENCE`
- `SUPPORTED_CAPTURE_MECHANISM`
- `POTENTIAL_CAPTURE`
- `DISPUTED_CAPTURE`
- `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`

## Rules

- Documented relationship does not automatically establish capture.
- Shared rhetoric does not automatically establish coordination.
- Institutional alignment does not automatically establish private intent.
- Preserve competing explanations and counterevidence that bear on the scoped claim.
- Trace mechanisms: appointments, funding, gatekeeping, agenda-setting, ownership, credentialing, regulatory authority, information control, platform power, coercion, dependency, or other evidenced levers.
