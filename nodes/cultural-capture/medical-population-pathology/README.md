# Medical Population Pathology — Cultural-Capture Investigation

This dossier tracks cases where a heterogeneous population, neurotype, disability class, or inherited/developmental phenotype is rhetorically compressed into a disease burden, social catastrophe, preventable defect, or singular causal target.

## Mechanisms to test

- diagnostic conflation
- comorbidity laundering
- burden/cost framing
- catastrophe or epidemic framing
- prevention versus treatment/support substitution
- research-agenda redirection
- authority amplification
- reproductive or screening implications
- displacement of present-life medical and support needs

## Boundary

This dossier does not presume eugenics, capture, or malicious intent. It records the structure of the rhetoric/policy pathway and asks which consequences are evidenced, which are plausible, and which remain unsupported.
